    Alice8/21/25About 5 minDuolingoEnglishDuolingoStoryB3

    The Wi-Fi-Free Office - B3

    A Review by Vikram

    A few days ago, I visited a campaign office. There was no Wi-Fi, but I had the best time! Let me tell you how it was:

    The office had no Wi-Fi, and that meant no internet. It was strange at first. How do they do their work? But then I realized, without Wi-Fi, people talk to each other a lot more! The candidates and their representatives have great discussions about important issues. It's like going back in time!

    I saw many campaign posters, but they were all handwritten! Without Wi-Fi, they couldn't print fancy signs. This made the campaign feel personal. I even joined in and wrote a sign to stand up for healthier foods! The candidates were happy to have help.

    Later, everyone gathered for a small barbecue outside. They grilled vegetables, and we all enjoyed plenty of laughter. No one checked their phones, and everyone was present. It was refreshing and wonderfully simple.

    Visiting this campaign office was a unique experience. Their lack of Wi-Fi made people friendlier and more connected. Additionally, it reminded me of the power of direct communication.

    Five stars for this campaign office!

    Chinese

    无 Wi-Fi 的办公室

    Vikram 的评论

    几天前，我参观了一个竞选办公室。那里没有 Wi-Fi，但我度过了最棒的时光！让我告诉你是怎样的：

    办公室没有 Wi-Fi，这意味着没有网络。一开始感觉很奇怪。他们怎么开展工作呢？但后来我意识到，没有 Wi-Fi，人们会更多地互相交谈！候选人和他们的代表们会就重要问题进行很好的讨论。这就像回到过去一样！

    我看到许多竞选海报，但它们都是手写的！没有 Wi-Fi，他们无法打印精美的标牌。这让竞选活动感觉更有个人色彩。我甚至加入进来，写了一张标语来支持更健康的食物！候选人们很高兴得到帮助。

    后来，大家聚在一起，在外面举行了一个小型烧烤。他们烤了蔬菜，我们都享受了许多欢笑。没有人看手机，大家都在场。这种感觉清新而美好、简单。

    参观这个竞选办公室是一次独特的体验。他们没有 Wi-Fi 让人们更加友好和紧密联系。此外，这让我想起了直接沟通的力量。

    给这个竞选办公室五星好评！

    Speaking & Writing

    🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

    2025-08-21

    How did the lack of Wi-Fi impact Vikram?

    Vikram realized that without Wi-Fi, people naturally interacted more, which made the whole campaign office feel warmer and more connected. He also noticed that the decorations were more personal, since everything was handwritten rather than printed. To him, this created a sense of authenticity and teamwork. Personally, I think the lack of Wi-Fi actually had a positive impact — it encouraged real conversations and made the campaign atmosphere more genuine and memorable.

    Vikram 意识到，没有 Wi-Fi 时，人们会更自然地互动，这让整个竞选办公室感觉更加温暖和紧密。他还注意到装饰更具个人特色，因为所有东西都是手写的，而不是打印的。对他来说，这带来了一种真实感和团队合作的氛围。就我个人而言，我认为缺少 Wi-Fi 实际上产生了积极的影响 — 它鼓励了真实的交流，并让竞选氛围更加真诚和难忘。

    Explanation:

    This version reaches Band 7+ because it:

    • Expands ideas with coherent linking (e.g., “which made,” “since,” “rather than”).
    • Uses varied vocabulary (“authenticity,” “genuine,” “memorable”).
    • Expresses personal opinion clearly (“Personally, I think…”).
    • Maintains a conversational but fluent tone, suitable for Speaking.

    🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

    2025-08-21

    How did the lack of Wi-Fi impact Vikram?

    The absence of Wi-Fi had a clear impact on Vikram’s experience at the campaign office. He observed that without an internet connection, people engaged in more meaningful discussions and paid greater attention to one another. This atmosphere created stronger bonds among the candidates and their representatives. In addition, the decorations appeared more personal, since posters were handwritten instead of computer-printed. Vikram interpreted this as a sign of authenticity and simplicity. Overall, the lack of Wi-Fi was not a limitation but rather a strength, as it highlighted the importance of face-to-face communication and human connection.

    缺少 Wi-Fi 对 Vikram 在竞选办公室的经历产生了明显的影响。他发现，没有网络时，人们会进行更有意义的讨论，并更加关注彼此。这种氛围让候选人和代表之间的关系更加紧密。此外，装饰看起来更有个人特色，因为海报是手写的，而不是电脑打印的。Vikram 将此理解为真实性和简洁的体现。总体而言，缺少 Wi-Fi 并不是限制，反而是一种优势，因为它突出了面对面交流和人际联系的重要性。

    Explanation:

    This version achieves Band 7+ because it:

    • Uses formal, precise vocabulary appropriate for Writing (“observed,” “interpreted,” “limitation,” “strength”).
    • Presents ideas in a clear logical order (observation → explanation → conclusion).
    • Avoids repetition and uses academic-style phrasing.
    • Develops ideas fully with specific examples from the story.
