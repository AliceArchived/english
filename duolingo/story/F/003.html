    Filming a Workout - B3

    Bea and Lin are in Bea's living room. There's a camera set up, fitness mats, and a list taped to the wall.

    • Bea: Alright, Lin. Are you ready?
    • Lin: Ready for what? What are we doing?
    • Bea: We're filming a workout vlog! I've planned it all. It's going to impress this fitness influencer I admire.
    • Lin: Oh, I just came for the free snacks.
    • Bea: Focus, Lin! We have to do this right. I prepared three workouts with perfect music and lighting.
    • Lin: You really planned everything…
    • Bea: Of course! Fitness is serious! It's not a joke.
    • Lin: Alright, alright. Let's start.

    Bea presses record on the camera. She starts doing fast jumping jacks.

    • Bea: Alright, Lin! Follow me! Jumping jacks, go!
    • Lin: Yeah, no. That looks hard.

    Lin sits on the mat and stretches lazily.

    • Bea: Lin! People want to see energy!
    • Lin: OK.

    Lin does one slow push-up and grins at the camera.

    • Lin: Hello, internet. This is my workout. Slow and calm.

    Bea groans and keeps jumping.

    A week later, they check the video on Bea's laptop.

    • Bea: What?! Lin, your part went viral!
    • Lin: Really? People liked my slow moves?
    • Bea: Yes! They're saying, 'Be like Lin!'
    • Lin: Guess people aren't ready for fast workouts.
    • Bea: I can't believe this…
    • Lin: Don't stress, Bea. Next time, we'll try yoga. That's slow too.

    Vocabulary

    • viral: 英 /'vaɪrəl/, 美 /'vaɪrəl/
      • adj. 滤过性毒菌引起的；滤过性毒菌的
    • go viral
      • 迅速传播：指一条信息、视频、图片等在互联网上迅速传播、被广泛分享的现象。
    • tape: 英 /teɪp/, 美 /teɪp/
      • n. 带子
        • 录音磁带；录像带
      • vt. 用带子捆起来
      • vt. & vi. 录音
      • n. [U] a long narrow strip of material with a sticky substance on one side that is used for sticking things together
        胶带；胶条:
        adhesive / sticky tape 黏胶带
      • verb.
        1. ~ sth (up) to fasten sth by sticking or tying it with tape  用胶带粘住；用带子系紧:
          Put it in a box and tape it up securely.
          把这个放进盒子里,再用带子系牢。
        2. [+adv. / prep.] to stick sth onto sth else using sticky tape    用胶带粘贴:
          Someone had taped a message on the door.
          有人用胶带把字条贴在了门上。
        3. (NAmE) ~ sth (up) to tie a bandage firmly around an injury or a wound 用绷带包扎:
          That's a nasty cut—come on, we'll get it all taped up.
          伤口很严重,快,咱们用绷带把它包扎起来。
    • jumping jacks - 开合跳
    • push-up - 俯卧撑

    拍摄健身视频

    Bea 和 Lin 在 Bea 的客厅里。那里有一台相机、健身垫，墙上还贴着一张清单。

    • Bea: 好了，Lin。你准备好了吗？
    • Lin: 准备好干嘛？我们要做什么？
    • Bea: 我们要拍健身视频博客！我都计划好了。这会让一个我很佩服的健身博主印象深刻。
    • Lin: 哦，我只是冲着免费的零食来的。
    • Bea: 专心点，Lin！我们必须做好。我准备了三个动作，还有完美的音乐和灯光。
    • Lin: 你真的把一切都计划好了…
    • Bea: 当然！健身是严肃的事！不是开玩笑的。
    • Lin: 好吧，好吧。我们开始吧。

    Bea 按下相机的录制键。她开始快速做开合跳。

    • Bea: 好了，Lin！跟着我！开合跳，开始！
    • Lin: 算了吧。那看起来太难了。

    Lin 坐在垫子上懒洋洋地伸展。

    • Bea: Lin！观众想看到活力！
    • Lin: 好吧。

    Lin 做了一个慢吞吞的俯卧撑，并对着镜头笑。

    • Lin: 大家好，这就是我的锻炼。慢而平静。

    Bea 抱怨着，继续跳。

    一周后，她们在 Bea 的笔记本电脑上查看视频。

    • Bea: 什么？！Lin，你的部分火了！
    • Lin: 真的？人们喜欢我的慢动作？
    • Bea: 是的！他们都在说，“像 Lin 一样！”
    • Lin: 看来人们还没准备好接受快节奏的锻炼。
    • Bea: 我简直不敢相信…
    • Lin: 别紧张，Bea。下次我们试试瑜伽吧。那也很慢。

    Sentences

    • Bea wants Lin to show more excitement.
    • Lin shows enthusiasm during the workout.
    • There's a camera set up, fitness mats, and a list taped to the wall.
      • 在 “There’s a camera set up” 和 “There’s a camera” 之间，其实是 语气和信息量不同。
        1. There’s a camera.
          • 只是单纯陈述：“这里有一台相机”。
          • 强调“存在”，没有描述状态或用途。
          • 中文就是：“有一台相机。”
        2. There’s a camera set up.
          • “set up” 在这里是 过去分词作形容词，意思是“架好 / 安装好 / 调整好”。
          • 强调这台相机 不仅存在，还处于准备好使用的状态。
          • 中文更自然的翻译：“这里架好了一台相机。”
            → 表达出相机已经摆放好，随时可以录影。
      • 故事语境里（拍摄健身 vlog），作者用 “There’s a camera set up”，就是想说明相机 已经架好并处于准备工作状态，不仅仅是“有一台相机”。

    Speaking & Writing

    🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

    2025-08-30

    Why does Lin's part of the video go viral?

    Lin’s part of the video goes viral because it is different from what people usually expect in workout videos. Instead of fast, intense movements, she does a slow push-up and talks to the camera in a calm, humorous way. Viewers find this unusual and relatable, so they comment things like “Be like Lin!” Her relaxed style makes people feel less pressured about exercise. As a result, Bea and Lin decide that their next vlog should focus on yoga, which also highlights slow and mindful movements.

    Lin 的视频片段会走红，是因为它和人们平时看到的健身视频不同。她没有做快速、激烈的动作，而是慢慢做俯卧撑，还用平静、幽默的方式对着镜头说话。观众觉得这种方式新奇又贴近生活，所以纷纷留言“像 Lin 一样！” 她这种轻松的风格让人们觉得锻炼没有压力。于是，Bea 和 Lin 决定她们下一个视频要专注于瑜伽，因为瑜伽也强调缓慢和专注的动作。

    Explanation:

    This version is Band 7+ for Speaking because:

    • It uses natural, conversational phrasing with varied sentence lengths.
    • It includes linking words like “instead of,” “so,” “as a result.”
    • It explains why people liked Lin’s part, not just what happened.
    • It has some emotional expression (“relatable,” “less pressured”), which makes it sound fluent and natural.

    🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

    2025-08-30

    Why does Lin's part of the video go viral?

    Lin’s part of the video goes viral mainly because it offers a refreshing contrast to traditional workout content. While Bea focuses on fast-paced and energetic exercises, Lin presents an alternative approach by performing a single slow push-up and greeting the audience calmly. Her unusual style resonates with viewers who may feel intimidated by overly intense fitness routines. The comments, such as “Be like Lin!”, reflect the audience’s appreciation of her relaxed and humorous presentation. This unexpected popularity encourages Bea and Lin to plan their next vlog on yoga, which similarly emphasizes slow, mindful, and accessible movements.

    Lin 的视频片段之所以会走红，主要是因为它与传统的健身内容形成了鲜明对比。当 Bea 专注于快速而充满活力的锻炼时，Lin 却以另一种方式出现——她只做了一个缓慢的俯卧撑，并平静地向观众问好。她这种不寻常的风格引起了观众的共鸣，尤其是那些对高强度锻炼感到畏惧的人。评论中“像 Lin 一样！”正好体现了观众对她轻松而幽默表现的喜爱。这种出乎意料的受欢迎程度，也促使 Bea 和 Lin 打算在下一个视频里拍瑜伽，因为瑜伽同样强调缓慢、专注和容易接受的动作。

    Explanation:

    This version is Band 7+ for Writing because:

    • It has a clear structure with topic sentence, supporting details, and conclusion.
    • Uses academic vocabulary (“refreshing contrast,” “resonates with viewers,” “appreciation,” “encourages”).
    • Sentences are longer and more formal than in speaking.
    • Cohesion is achieved through connectors like “while,” “mainly because,” “this unexpected popularity.”
    • resonate: 英 /'rezəneɪt/, 美 /'rezəneɪt/
      • v. （嗓音、乐器等）产生共鸣；发出回响；回荡
        • （使）回响，起回声
        • 使产生联想；引起共鸣；和…的想法（或观念）类似
    • intimidate: 英 /ɪn'tɪmɪdeɪt/, 美 /ɪn'tɪmɪdeɪt/
      • vt. 恐吓, 威胁

    refreshing contrast

    • refreshing = pleasantly surprising, new, different in a positive way.
    • contrast = a difference between two things.

    So “a refreshing contrast” = a difference that feels positive and enjoyable, usually because it breaks away from something boring, repetitive, or too intense.

    Native-like Examples

    • Her calm teaching style is a refreshing contrast to the usual strict classroom atmosphere.
    • The small family-owned café was a refreshing contrast to the crowded chain restaurants nearby.
    • Lin’s slow workout created a refreshing contrast to Bea’s fast and intense routine.

    👉 All three sound very natural — natives use this in daily speech, essays, reviews, and even journalism.

    Where natives often use it

    • Food/experience: The cold lemonade was a refreshing contrast to the hot weather.
    • People/behavior: His honesty was a refreshing contrast to the usual political talk.
    • Ideas/work: The author’s humor was a refreshing contrast to the seriousness of the topic.

    Alternatives natives also use

    • a welcome change
    • a striking difference
    • a pleasant surprise
    • a breath of fresh air (more idiomatic, especially in speaking)

    So in IELTS:

    • In Speaking, “a refreshing contrast” is a bit formal — natives might more often say “a nice change” or “a breath of fresh air”.
    • In Writing, “a refreshing contrast” is excellent — it sounds academic yet natural.

    resonates

    Sentence:
    Her unusual style resonates with viewers who may feel intimidated by overly intense fitness routines.

    Meaning of resonates
    • Resonate (verb) comes from the idea of sound vibrating strongly.
    • In everyday English, it means “to connect deeply” or “to have a strong emotional impact on someone.”

    So here:

    • “Her unusual style resonates with viewers” = Viewers strongly relate to her style.
    • It feels meaningful, familiar, or appealing to them.
    Why it works here
    • The audience feels that Lin’s slow, relaxed workout matches their own feelings or needs.
    • Since many people find intense workouts scary (intimidating), Lin’s calm approach feels comfortable. That emotional connection is what “resonates” describes.
    Alternatives you can use instead of resonates (with different tones):
    • connects with → simpler, good for IELTS Speaking.
    • appeals to → neutral, common in both writing and speaking.
    • strikes a chord with → idiomatic, good for higher-level Speaking.
    • is relatable to → more informal, natural for Speaking.

    ✅ So, if we rewrite:

    • Her unusual style resonates with viewers…
      = Her unusual style connects with viewers…
      = Her unusual style appeals to viewers…
      = Her unusual style strikes a chord with viewers…

    Speaking vs Writing Feature Comparison

    2025-08-30

    FeatureSpeaking VersionWriting Version
    Sentence lengthMostly medium (10–15 words), some shortLonger (20+ words), more complex structures
    ToneConversational, friendly, slightly humorousFormal, objective, academic-style
    Common connectors“so,” “as a result,” “instead of”“while,” “mainly because,” “this unexpected popularity”
    Vocabulary choiceEveryday words (“relaxed,” “less pressured”)Higher-level words (“refreshing contrast,” “resonates,” “emphasizes”)
    Emotional expressionDirect, personal (“relatable,” “feel less pressured”)Limited, indirect, more neutral tone
    Rhetorical/detail styleExplains reasons with simple cause–effect logicStructured argument with contrast and examples

    Practice History

    2025-08-30

    Why does Lin's part of the video go viral?

    Helpful Phrases: Lin's part/ people enjoy/ different from

    Lin's part of the video is popular. People enjoy slow exercises and commented 'Be like Lin!' That makes Bea and Lin to plan another vlog about yoga, because it is slow.

    Corrections:

    1. “is popular” → better as “becomes popular” to show change over time.
    2. “That makes Bea and Lin to plan” → remove “to” → “makes Bea and Lin plan”.
    3. Add quotation marks for the comment.
    4. “because it is slow” → more natural as “because it is also slow”.

    Lin's part of the video becomes popular. People enjoy the slow exercises and commented, "Be like Lin!" That makes Bea and Lin plan another vlog about yoga, because it is also slow.

    Lin 在视频中的部分变得很受欢迎。人们喜欢这种慢动作锻炼，并评论说：“像 Lin 一样！” 这让 Bea 和 Lin 计划拍另一个关于瑜伽的视频，因为它也很慢。
