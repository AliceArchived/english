    3. Plan

    1. I can't wait for the holiday!
    2. 你想要去什么地方吗？
      1. Where do you want to go?
        1. sounds more natural and is more commonly used in English.
      2. Do you want to go anywhere?
        1. correct, but it may sound slightly less natural because the use of "anywhere" suggests that the speaker is unsure of where the listener would like to go or if they want to go anywhere at all.
    3. 多儿这周四会返回美国。
      1. Duo will return to the US this Thursday. (on)
