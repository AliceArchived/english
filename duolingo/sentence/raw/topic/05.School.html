    10. 如果答案不正确，就不要打勾**。**

      1. “check” can also mean “to verify” or “to examine,” which could cause confusion in this context.
    12. If the answer isn't correct, don't put a check.
      1. In English, “put a check” is a more common and idiomatic way of expressing the action of marking a correct answer with a checkmark.
    13. 他这周有一个测试，所以他必须学习。
      1. He has a test this week, so he must study.
      2. He has a test this week, so he has to study.
      3. The phrase “has to” is a stronger way of indicating that something is necessary or required, whereas “must” can be interpreted as a personal opinion or recommendation.
