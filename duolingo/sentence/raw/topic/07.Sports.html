    7. Sports

    1. General

    1. 我的孙女跑得像猫一样快！
      1. My granddaughter runs as quickly as a cat!
    2. I'm bad at dancing.
    3. 在山里徒步旅行很棒。
      1. Hiking in the mountains is wonderful.
    4. 咱们一起去攀岩吧。
      1. Let's go rock climbing together!
    5. 她会打排球，如果她休一天假的话。
      1. She will play volleyball, if she takes a day off.
    6. If it doesn't rain, she'll play volleyball outside.
    7. 多儿经常在他的空闲时间去健身房。
      1. Duo often goes to the gym in his free time.
    8. 你的预约是在早上还是在下午？
      1. Is your appointment in the morning or in the afternoon?
    9. 我们上周日去冲浪了。
      1. We went surfing last Sunday.
    10. 如果天气好的话，咱们去冲浪吧。
      1. If the weather is nice, let's go surfing.
    11. 我确定我们可以去布鲁克林大桥上骑自行车。
      1. I'm sure we can go cycling on the Brooklyn Bridge.
    12. 网球比赛在纽约大学吗？
      1. Is the tennis match at NYU?
    13. We'll go surfing if we have enough time.
    14. If the weather is hot and sunny, he'll go surfing.
    15. Let's go skiing.
      1. 滑雪
    16. 咱们一起放风筝吧。
      1. Let's fly a kite together!
    17. I don't want to fly a kite because I don't like the wind.
    18. If it isn't windy, we can't fly a kite.
    19. 你明天想要去滑冰吗？
      1. Do you want to go ice skating tomorrow?
    20. 这场足球比赛会很好玩。
      1. This soccer game will be fun!
      2. This soccer match will be fun!
    21. I watched a hockey game at the stadium last night.
    22. I'm sure we can go ice skating in Central Park.
    23. We can watch a soccer match this weekend.
    24. 我因该少坐沙发，多去健身房。
      1. I should sit on the sofa less and go to the gym more.
    25. If it doesn't rain, she'll play volleyball outside.
    26. If it rains a lot, we might not be doing any activities.
    27. I kicked the ball near the goal, but it didn't go inside.
    28. 她用她的右手接住了球。
      1. She caught the ball with her right hand.
    29. We're going swimming, would you like to join us.
    30. If you're afraid of deep water, don't go swimming?
    31. Why don't they go swimming in the lake.
    32. I'm going fishing by myself.
    33. 你要去游泳，不是吗？
      1. You're going swimming, aren't you?
    34. 你今天下午要去钓鱼，不是吗？
      1. You're going fishing this afternoon, aren't you?
    35. We're going swimming, would you like to join us?
    36. Do you prefer to go hiking or rock climbing?
    37. I'm going fishing by myself.
    38. Why don't you go fishing with us?
    39. 你想要在外面打排球吗？
      1. Would you like to play volleyball outside?
    40. 她骑她的自行车去野餐。
      1. She rode her bike to the picnic. (for a picnic)

    2. Sports

    2.1 game

    1. When was the big game?
    2. The big game was crazy yesterday!
    3. We're ready for the game!
    4. 总是站在球门前面的运动员是谁？
      1. Who is the player that always stands in front of the goal?
    5. 谁吧足球踢进了球门里？
      1. Who kicked the soccer ball into the goal.
    6. 你喜欢排球还是篮球？
      1. Do you like volleyball or basketball?
    7. 我们现在在海滩上打排球。
      1. We're playing volleyball at the beach now.
    8. 他擅长美式橄榄球，但是他不擅长足球。
      1. He's good at American football, but he's bad at soccer.
    9. 我不擅长足球，但是我擅长棒球。
      1. I am bad at soccer, but I'm good at baseball.

    2.2 Dance

    1. What time is his dance lesson?
    2. I don't want to take dance lessons.
    3. 如果你上舞蹈课，你会把舞跳得更好！
      1. If you take dance lessons, you'll get better at dancing.
    4. 艾玛一周有三次舞蹈课。
      1. Emma has dance lessons three times a week.
    5. I want to get better at dancing.

    2.3 Cycling

    1. I always enjoy cycling.
    2. If it doesn't rain, we'll go cycling on the Brooklyn Bridge.
    3. Cycling on the Brooklyn Bridge is fun.

    2.4 Hiking

    1. Don't go hiking by yourself.
    2. 她更喜欢去徒步旅行。
      1. She prefers to go hiking.
    3. 你这个周末要去野营，不是吗？
      1. You're going camping this weekend, aren't you?
    4. 你更喜欢去徒步旅行还是攀岩？
      1. Do you prefer to go hiking or rock climbing?
    5. Why don't they go rock climbing together?
    6. Be careful of bears during the hike.
    7. During the hike, be careful of wild animals.
    8. Be careful of dangerous animals during the hike.
    9. If you see a monkey on the hike you can take a photo of it.
    10. 她要不要去徒步旅行？
      1. Is she going hiking, or not?
    11. 你多久去一次徒步旅行？
      1. How often do you go hiking?
    12. 我们可以在明天早上八点开始徒步旅行吗？
      1. Can we begin hiking at 8 a.m. tomorrow?
    13. Why do you hate hiking?
    14. 我的孩子们享受野营。
      1. My children enjoy camping. (camp)

    2.5 Skiiing

    1. 如果下雪，我们会去滑雪。
      1. If it snows, we'll go skiing.

    2.6 Swim

    1. 孩子们上周每天都游泳。
      1. The children go swimming every day last week.
        1. emphasize the act of going to the pool or beach
      2. The children swam every day last week.
        1. emphasize the activity of swimming

    3. Sports Actions

    1. 那一脚踢得真棒！
      1. That was a great kick.
    2. 他没有接住球。
      1. He didn't catch the ball.
    3. 她把球扔给了别人。
      1. She threw the ball to someone else.
      2. throw, threw, thrown, throwing, throws
    4. 接球，本。
      1. Catch the ball, Ben.
    5. 噢不，那一脚踢得很糟糕。
      1. Oh no, that was a terrible kick.
    6. 他把球踢进了球门。
      1. He kicked the ball into the goal!
    7. 他把棒球扔给了我。
      1. He threw the baseball to me.
    8. 他在扔东西吗？
      1. Is he throwing something?
    9. 谁接住了球？
      1. Who caught the ball?
    10. 她把球扔得非常远。
      1. She threw the ball very far!
    11. 你总是用你的左手扔球吗？
      1. Do you always throw the ball with your left hand?
    12. 踢得真棒，丽莎！
      1. Great kick, Lisa!
    13. 狗在接球。
      1. The dog is catching the ball.
    14. 我接住了球， 然后我的对赢了比赛。
      1. I caught the ball and then my team won the game.
    15. She caught the ball with her right hand.
    16. 接球，快点！
      1. Catch the ball, hurry.
    17. Throw the ball to Anna, hurry!
    18. I kicked the ball near the goal, but it didn't go inside.
    19. He kicked the ball to me, and then he fell down.
    20. She threw the ball to the wrong person.
    21. She catches the baseball, and then she fell down.

    3. Stadium

    1. 我们昨晚在体育场。
      1. We were at the stadium last night.
    2. 我们半夜从体育场回来。
      1. We got back from the stadium at midnight.
    3. 我们每周都在体育场打曲棍球。
      1. We play hockey at the stadium every weekend. (play the hockey)
    4. 昨天在体育场有许多人。
      1. There were a lot of people at the stadium yesterday.
    5. 我们上周五在体育场看了一场足球比赛。
      1. We watched a soccer game at the stadium last Friday.

    4. Competion

    1. 昨晚的足球比赛进行得怎么样？
      1. How did the soccer game go last night?
    2. 昨晚的比赛进行得还可以。
      1. The game went OK last night.
    3. 你昨天的篮球比赛进行得顺利吗？
      1. Did your basketball game go well yesterday?
    4. How did the hockey game go last Friday?
    5. 你很享受比赛吗？
      1. Do you enjoy competitions?
    6. 你为大赛做好准备了吗？
      1. Are you ready for the big game?
    7. 足球队昨天在雨中打比赛吗？
      1. Did the soccer team play the game in the rain yesterday?
    8. 他们昨天在雨中打曲棍球吗？
      1. Were they playing hockey in the rain yesterday?
    9. 昨天的棒球比赛进行得顺利。
      1. The baseball game went well yesterday.
    10. 曲棍球比赛进行得非常顺利。
      1. The hockey game went very well.
    11. 他们昨晚在雨中打了比赛。
      1. They were playing the game in the rain last night.
    12. We were watching the game in the rain all night.
    13. Is your team ready for the game?
    14. 你赢了吗？
      1. Have you win?
        1. “Have you win?” is incorrect because it uses the present perfect tense, which is not suitable for talking about a specific completed action in the past.
      2. Did you win?
        1. the simple past tense is used to talk about completed actions in the past.
