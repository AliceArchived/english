      1. With just a few positive changes, you too can stop struggling and be happy.
      2. The placement of "too" depends on emphasis and sentence flow. Here's why it's positioned where it is in your sentence:
        1. Emphasis:
          1. When "too" appears in the middle, as in "you too can stop struggling," it emphasizes that "you" (the reader) are included in the group being addressed, implying others have already succeeded in this transformation.
          2. If "too" is placed at the end, as in "you can stop struggling and be happy too," the emphasis shifts to the action of being happy, suggesting that happiness is an additional outcome.
        2. Natural Flow:
          1. In the original sentence, the focus is on encouraging the reader (you). Placing "too" immediately after "you" highlights that the statement is directed specifically at them.
          2. Moving "too" to the end might slightly alter the rhythm or reduce the personal emphasis.
