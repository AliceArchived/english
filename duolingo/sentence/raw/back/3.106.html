      1. The illusion with the fire was amazing.
      2. Why use "with":
        1. "with" means the illusion involved or used fire as a tool, element, or feature.
        2. It implies: There was an illusion, and fire was part of how it worked.
        3. Example:
          1. The trick with the cards was incredible. → 表示这个魔术是用“扑克牌”来做的。
          2. The illusion with the fire → 是说这个幻象“用到了火”。
        4. Why not use "of":
          1. "of the fire" implies the fire itself is the illusion. That changes the meaning.
          2. The illusion of the fire = 这个火是假的，火本身是幻觉。
          3. 而原句并不是在说“火是假的”，而是说“这个用了火的幻象很惊艳”。
