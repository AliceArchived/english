      2. Direct speech is when you say their exact words.

        1. Alex said, "I love music."

      3. Indirect speech is when you don't use their exact words. Using that is optional.

        1. Alex said that he loved music.
        2. Alex said he loved music.

      4. When you use a past verb, like said or thought, the verb in the phrase usually changes to the past too.

        1. Mary said, "I'm working."
        2. Mary said she was working.
        3. Mary thought, "Ben worked hard."
        4. Mary thought Ben had worked hard.
