      1. If you buy an item, and it doesn't do what it's supposed to do, you might say, "It's no good!"
      2. it's no good: it doesn't work correctly.
      3. "It's no good"
        1. Implies uselessness or failure (it doesn't work as intended).
        2. Often expresses disappointment or frustration with something that is supposed to function a certain way but doesn’t.
        3. Example:
          1. "I tried fixing the phone, but it's no good." (It’s useless; it still doesn’t work.)
          2. "This pen is no good—it doesn’t write!" (It’s completely ineffective.)
      4. "It's not good"
        1. Simply means something is of low quality or not satisfactory but doesn't necessarily mean it's completely useless.
        2. More general and neutral than "It's no good."
        3. Example:
          1. "This movie is not good." (It’s bad, but it still exists as a movie.)
          2. "The soup is not good; it's too salty." (It could still be eaten but isn’t tasty.)
