    4. Discuss etiquette - 7-19

    讨论礼仪

    etiquette 英 /ˈetɪkət/, 美 /ˈetɪkət/

    1. The host is the person who invites the guests and provides the place for an event.
      1. host: someone who organizes a party
    2. The host needs to greet the guests.
    3. If you want to know whether a restaurant has vegetarian food, call them to inquire about it.
      1. inquire: ask for information
    4. 也许如果主持人让我们这么做的话。 1. Maybe if the host asks us to. 2. "maybe if" is not weird. It is a common and natural expression used to indicate a conditional possibility or scenario. The phrase suggests that something might happen or be true under a certain condition. 3. Explanation: 1. "Maybe" introduces uncertainty or possibility. 2. "If" sets up the condition that makes the possibility plausible. 4. Example Sentences: 1. Maybe if I work harder, I'll get the promotion. 2. Maybe if you explain your reasons, they'll understand. 3. 也许如果我更努力工作，我就能得到晋升。 4. 也许如果你解释一下原因，他们会理解。
    5. On the invitation, it says, "RSVP by April 4th." So we have to let them know by then whether we can attend or not.
      1. RAVP: respond to an invitation
    6. If you're planning to go to the party, you'll need to RSVP by this week!
