    A song can be called "clever" when it shows wit, creativity, or smart wordplay in its lyrics, structure, or concept. In the sentence:

    • "Oscar's song was clever and funny."

    “Clever” likely refers to one or more of the following:

    • Wordplay or puns — the lyrics might include double meanings or rhymes that surprise or amuse the listener.
    • Unusual structure — perhaps the song presents an idea in a unique way.
    • Satire or irony — the song could smartly poke fun at something or tell a story in an unexpected manner.

    In short, calling a song "clever" is praising the intelligence and creativity behind it.
