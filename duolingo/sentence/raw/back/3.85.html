      1. He never goes to class.
      2. Why "class" is used in the sentence:
        1. "He never goes to class."
        2. Here, "class" is used as an uncountable noun, meaning the general concept of attending lessons.
        3. This phrasing is common when talking about someone's attendance habit in school or university.
        4. Example:
          1. "I have to go to class now." → Refers to attending lessons in general.
      3. When to use "classes":
        1. "He never goes to classes."
        2. "Classes" (plural) refers to individual lessons or courses.
        3. This phrasing suggests he skips multiple specific classes rather than just generally not attending school.
        4. Example:
          1. "I signed up for three classes this semester." → Refers to specific courses.
      4. Summary:
        1. ✅ "He never goes to class." → General habit of not attending school/lectures.
        2. ✅ "He never goes to classes." → Emphasizes skipping multiple specific courses.
