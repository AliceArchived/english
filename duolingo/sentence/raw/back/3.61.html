      1. We found out that the house we bought has some plumbing issues, so we'll have to call in a professional.
      2. The phrase "call in" has a few meanings depending on the context. Here are the main uses:
        1. Request Assistance: When you "call in" someone, it means to request their help or services, usually from a professional or expert. For example, "We need to call in a plumber to fix the leak."
        2. Report or Communicate: In a work context, to "call in" can mean to communicate typically by phone, often used when someone reports they will not be able to attend work due to sickness or other reasons. For instance, "She called in sick today."
        3. Summon: It can also mean to summon someone, especially to deal with an emergency or urgent issue. For example, "The manager had to call in extra staff for the busy weekend."
