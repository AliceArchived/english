    11. 拍完照后，我们回到了里面。
      1. After being photographed, we went back inside.(been)
      2. The word "being" is correct in this context because it indicates an action that was happening to the subject (you) at that time. Here's why "been" wouldn't work:
        1. "Being" refers to the process or state of something happening, often in the present participle form (e.g., being photographed, being watched). In this case, it shows that the action of photographing was done to you.
        2. "Been" is the past participle of "be" and is generally used in perfect tenses (e.g., have been, had been). It implies a completed state rather than the process of an action occurring.
        3. For example:
          1. "After being photographed" = After the process of taking the photograph.
          2. "After been photographed" = Grammatically incorrect because "been" requires an auxiliary verb like "have" (e.g., "After having been photographed").
