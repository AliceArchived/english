      1. "I'd love to go to the bar with you, but I don't drink alcohol."
        This sentence uses 'I'd,' which means 'I would.' It implies a willingness or desire to go to the bar, but with a caveat that the speaker doesn't drink alcohol.
      2. "I love to go to the bar with you, but I don't drink alcohol."
        This sentence uses 'I love,' which indicates a habitual enjoyment or liking for going to the bar with the person. However, it still mentions that the speaker doesn't drink alcohol.
      3. In summary, 'I'd love' expresses a current desire or inclination, whereas 'I love' indicates an ongoing or habitual enjoyment.
