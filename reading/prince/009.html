    Chapter 09

    Alice8/3/25About 10 minWriting小王子

    Chapter 09

    He found himself in the neighborhood of the asteroids 325, 326, 327, 328, 329, and 330.
    他发现自己来到了 325、326、327、328、329 和 330 号小行星的附近。

    He began, therefore, by visiting them, in order to add to his knowledge.
    于是他开始访问这些小行星，以增长自己的见识。

    The first of them was inhabited by a king.
    第一颗小行星上住着一位国王。

    Clad in royal purple and ermine, he was seated upon a throne which was at the same time both simple and majestic.
    他身披紫色王袍和貂皮，坐在一张既朴素又庄严的宝座上。

    "Ah! Here is a subject," exclaimed the king, when he saw the little prince coming.
    当他看见小王子走来时，国王喊道：“啊，来了一个臣民！”

    And the little prince asked himself: "How could he recognize me when he had never seen me before?"
    小王子心想：“他从来没有见过我，怎么会认得我呢？”

    He did not know how the world is simplified for kings. To them, all men are subjects.
    他不知道，对于国王来说，世界是非常简单的：所有的人都是臣民。

    "Approach, so that I may see you better," said the king, who felt consumingly proud of being at last a king over somebody.
    国王因为终于成了某个人的国王而自豪万分，他对小王子说道：“靠近些，好让我看得更清楚。”

    The little prince looked everywhere to find a place to sit down; but the entire planet was crammed and obstructed by the king's magnificent ermine robe.
    小王子环顾四周，想找个地方坐下，可是整个星球都被国王那件华丽的貂皮长袍占满了。

    So he remained standing upright, and, since he was tired, he yawned.
    他只好继续站着，可是因为疲倦了，他打起了哈欠。

    "It is contrary to etiquette to yawn in the presence of a king," the monarch said to him. "I forbid you to do so."
    国王对他说：“在国王面前打哈欠是违反礼节的。我禁止你打哈欠。”

    "I can't help it. I can't stop myself," replied the little prince, thoroughly embarrassed.
    小王子十分不好意思地说道：“我实在忍不住，我没法克制自己。”

    "I have come on a long journey, and I have had no sleep."
    “我长途跋涉来到这里，还没有睡觉呢。”

    "Ah, then," the king said. "I order you to yawn. It is years since I have seen anyone yawning."
    国王说：“那好吧，我命令你打哈欠。我已经好多年没有见过有人打哈欠了。”

    "Yawns, to me, are objects of curiosity. Come, now! Yawn again! It is an order."
    “打哈欠对我来说是一件新奇的事。来吧，现在！再打个哈欠！这是命令。”

    "That frightens me… I cannot, any more…" murmured the little prince, now completely abashed.
    “这倒吓到我了……我打不出哈欠来了。”小王子红着脸嘟囔道。

    "Hum! Hum!" replied the king. "Then I — I order you sometimes to yawn and sometimes to—"
    “嗯！嗯！”国王回答道：“那么我——我命令你有时候打哈欠，有时候又……”

    He sputtered a little, and seemed vexed.
    他嘟嘟囔囔，显得有点恼火。

    For what the king fundamentally insisted upon was that his authority should be respected.
    因为国王所坚持的，主要是保持他的威严受到尊敬。

    He tolerated no disobedience.
    他不能容忍违抗命令。

    He was an absolute monarch.
    他是一位专制的君主。

    But, because he was a very good man, he made his orders reasonable.
    可是，因为他很善良，他下的命令总是合理的。

    "If I ordered a general to change himself into a sea bird, and if the general did not obey me, that would not be the fault of the general. It would be my fault."
    他常常说：“如果我命令一位将军变成一只海鸟，而这位将军没有服从，这就不是将军的过错，而是我的过错。”

    "May I sit down?" came now a timid inquiry from the little prince.
    小王子腼腆地试探道：“我可以坐下吗？”

    "I order you to do so," the king answered him, and majestically gathered in a fold of his ermine mantle.
    “我命令你坐下。”国王回答道，同时庄重地收拢了一下他那件貂皮长袍。

    But the little prince was wondering…
    可是小王子心里觉得很奇怪……

    The planet was tiny. Over what could this king really rule?
    这颗行星那么小，这位国王究竟能统治什么呢？

    "Sire," he said to him, "I beg that you will excuse my asking you a question—"
    他对国王说：“陛下，请原谅，我想问您一个问题——”

    "I order you to ask me a question," the king hastened to assure him.
    国王急忙说道：“我命令你问我问题。”

    "Sire—over what do you rule?"
    “陛下——您统治什么呢？”

    "Over everything," said the king, with magnificent simplicity.
    国王非常简洁而威严地回答：“我统治一切。”

    "Over everything?"
    “一切？”

    The king made a gesture, which took in his planet, the other planets, and all the stars.
    国王做了一个手势，把他的行星、其他行星以及所有的星星都包括在内。

    "Over all that?" asked the little prince.
    小王子问道：“统治这一切吗？”

    "Over all that," the king answered.
    “统治这一切。”国王回答。

    For his rule was not only absolute: it was also universal.
    因为他的统治不仅是绝对的，而且是普遍的。

    "And the stars obey you?"
    “那么，这些星星都服从您吗？”

    "Certainly they do," the king said. "They obey instantly. I do not permit insubordination."
    “那当然！”国王说道，“它们立刻就得服从。我是不允许有违抗的。”

    Such power was a thing for the little prince to marvel at.
    这样的权力令小王子惊叹不已。

    If he had been master of such complete authority, he would have been able to watch the sunset, not forty-four times in one day, but seventy-two, or even a hundred, or even two hundred times, without ever having to move his chair.
    如果他掌握了这样彻底的权力，他就能看到日落，不是一日四十四次，而是七十二次，甚至一百次、二百次，也不需要搬动椅子。

    And because he felt a bit sad as he remembered his little planet, which he had forsaken, he plucked up his courage to ask the king a favour:
    他想起了自己遗弃的小星球，心里有点难过，于是鼓起勇气向国王提出一个请求：

    "I should like to see a sunset… do me that kindness… Order the sun to set…"
    “我想看日落……请您行个好，命令太阳落山吧……”

    "If I ordered a general to fly from one flower to another like a butterfly, or to write a tragic drama, or to change himself into a sea bird, and if the general did not carry out the order that he had received, which one of us would be in the wrong? The general, or myself?" the king demanded.
    国王说：“如果我命令一位将军像蝴蝶一样从一朵花飞到另一朵花，或者命令他写一出悲剧，或者变成一只海鸟，而这位将军没有执行，那么，是他不对，还是我不对呢？”

    "You," said the little prince firmly.
    “那当然是您的不对。”小王子坚定地回答。

    "Exactly. One must require from each one the duty which each one can perform," the king went on.
    “一点也不错，”国王接着说，“向每个人提出的要求应该是他们力所能及的。”

    "Accepted authority rests first of all on reason.
    “权威首先应该建立在理性的基础上。

    If you ordered your people to go and throw themselves into the sea, they would rise up in revolution.
    如果你命令百姓们去投海，他们就会起来造反。

    I have the right to require obedience because my orders are reasonable."
    我的命令是合理的，所以我才有权要求别人服从。”

    "Then my sunset?" the little prince reminded him: for he never forgot a question once he had asked it.
    “那么我的日落呢？”小王子提醒道——因为他一旦提出一个问题，就绝不会忘记。

    "You shall have your sunset. I shall command it.
    “你会看到日落的。我会命令它。”

    But, according to my science of government, I shall wait until conditions are favourable."
    “不过，按照我的治国学，我得等到条件成熟的时候。”

    "When will that be?" inquired the little prince.
    小王子问道：“那会是什么时候呢？”

    "Hum! Hum!" replied the king; and before saying anything else he consulted a bulky almanac.
    “嗯！嗯！”国王回答道；在说出别的话之前，他先翻阅了一本厚厚的历书。

    "Hum! Hum! That will be about—about—that will be this evening about twenty minutes to eight. And you will see how well I am obeyed."
    “嗯！嗯！那大约……会在今晚七点四十分！到时候你将看到我的命令是如何被服从的。”

    The little prince yawned.
    小王子打了个哈欠。

    He was regretting his lost sunset. And then, too, he was already beginning to be a little bored.
    他为错过日落而感到遗憾，而且他已经开始觉得有些厌烦。

    "I have nothing more to do here," he said to the king.
    他对国王说：“我在这儿没有什么更多的事情可做了。”

    "So I shall set out on my way again."
    “所以我应该重新启程了。”

    "Do not go," said the king, who was very proud of having a subject.
    国王说道：“别走。”他因为终于有了一个臣民而显得十分骄傲。

    "Do not go. I will make you a Minister!"
    “别走，我任命你当大臣。”

    "Minister of what?"
    “什么大臣？”

    "Minister of—of Justice!"
    “嗯……司法大臣！”

    "But there is nobody here to judge!"
    “可是，这儿一个要审判的人都没有啊！”

    "We do not know that," the king said to him.
    “我们还不能肯定这一点，”国王说道。

    "I have not yet made a complete tour of my kingdom. I am very old. There is no room here for a carriage. And it tires me to walk."
    “我年纪大了，这个地方又小，没有容得下銮驾的地方，再说一走路我就累。所以我还没有巡视过整个王国呢！”

    "Oh, but I have looked already!" said the little prince, turning around to give one more glance to the other side of the planet. On that side, as on this, there was nobody at all…
    “噢！可是我已经看过了。”小王子说道，并探身朝星球的另一边望去。那一边，就像这一边一样，一个人也没有……

    "Then you shall judge yourself," the king answered. "That is the most difficult thing of all.
    “那么你就审判你自己吧！”国王回答道。“这是最难的事情。”

    It is much more difficult to judge oneself than to judge others.
    “审判自己比审判别人要难得多。”

    If you succeed in judging yourself rightly, then you are indeed a man of true wisdom."
    “如果你能正确地审判自己，那么你就是一个真正聪明的人。”

    "Yes," said the little prince, "but I can judge myself anywhere. I do not need to live on this planet."
    “是的，”小王子说道，“在任何地方我都可以审判自己。我没有必要留在这个星球上。”

    "Hum! Hum!" said the king. "I have good reason to believe that somewhere on my planet there is an old rat.
    “嗯……嗯……”国王说道，“我有理由相信，在我的星球上住着一只老耗子。

    I hear him at night.
    夜里，我能听见它的声音。

    You can judge this old rat. From time to time you will condemn him to death.
    你可以审判这只老耗子，不时地判它死刑。

    Thus his life will depend on your justice.
    因此，它的生命将取决于你的判决。

    But you will pardon him on each occasion; for he must be treated thriftily. He is the only one we have."
    可是，你每次都要赦免它，要节制一些，因为我们只有这一只老耗子。”

    "I," replied the little prince, "do not like to condemn anyone to death. And now I think I will go on my way."
    “我不喜欢判处任何人死刑。我想我还是该走了。”小王子回答道。

    "No," said the king.
    “不行。”国王说道。

    But the little prince, having now completed his preparations for departure, had no wish to grieve the old monarch.
    但是，小王子已经准备好要出发了，他不想让这位老君主难过。

    "If Your Majesty wishes to be promptly obeyed," he said, "he should be able to give me a reasonable order.
    “如果陛下想要立刻得到服从，就应该给我一个合理的命令。”他说道。

    "He should be able, for example, to order me to be gone by the end of one minute. It seems to me that conditions are favourable."
    “比如说，您可以命令我在一分钟之内必须离开。我觉得条件已经成熟了。”

    As the king made no answer, the little prince hesitated a moment. Then, with a sigh, he took his leave.
    国王没有回答。小王子犹豫了一下，接着叹了口气，就告辞离开了。

    "I made you my Ambassador," the king called out, hastily.
    “我任命你当我的大使！”国王慌忙喊道。

    He had a magnificent air of authority.
    他显出一副威严的样子。

    "The grown-ups are very strange," the little prince said to himself, as he continued on his journey.
    “这些大人真奇怪。”小王子一边继续他的旅行，一边自言自语。
