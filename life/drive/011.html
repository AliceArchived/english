    Test G1

    01

    As a G1 driver, which highways or expressways are you not allowed to drive on?

    1. Highways with more than two lanes
    2. Highways with speed limits above 100 km/h
    3. 400-series highways or expressways, including The Gardiner Expressway, Don Valley Parkway, and The Queen Elizabeth Way (QEW)
    4. Local roads only

    作为 G1 驾驶员，你不能驾驶在哪些高速公路或快速路上？

    1. 有两个以上车道的高速公路
    2. 限速超过 100 公里/小时的高速公路
    3. 400 系列高速公路或快速路，包括嘉迪纳高速（Gardiner Expressway）、唐谷公园道（Don Valley Parkway）、伊丽莎白女王道（QEW）
    4. 仅限本地道路
    • English Explanation:
      As a G1 driver in Ontario, you are prohibited from driving on 400-series highways and certain high-speed expressways (such as the Gardiner, DVP(Don Valley Parkway,), and QEW(The Queen Elizabeth Way)). These roads are considered too fast and dangerous for beginner drivers. You are still allowed to drive on local roads, city streets, and smaller highways that are not restricted, as long as you follow all G1 rules (accompanied by a fully licensed driver with 4+ years experience, zero alcohol, no midnight–5 a.m. driving, etc.).
    • 中文解释：
      在安省，G1 驾驶员禁止在 400 系列高速公路和部分高速度的快速路（如嘉迪纳高速、唐谷公园道、伊丽莎白女王道）上驾驶。这些道路车速过快、车流量大，对新手驾驶员风险太高。你仍然可以在本地道路、城市街道以及没有受限的小型高速公路上开车，但必须遵守 G1 的规定（副驾驶必须有 4 年以上全牌驾龄、驾驶员零酒精、禁止午夜到凌晨 5 点开车等）。

    🚗 Ontario G1 Knowledge Test – Key Rules

    （安省 G1 笔试高频考点清单）

    1. Driving Restrictions 驾驶限制

    • G1 drivers must always drive with a fully licensed driver (G, 4+ years experience) in the front passenger seat.
      G1 驾驶员必须有一位持全牌且有 4 年以上驾龄的司机坐在副驾驶。
    • Zero blood alcohol level is required for the driver.
      驾驶员必须保持零酒精含量。
    • No driving between midnight and 5 a.m.
      禁止在午夜 12 点至凌晨 5 点之间驾驶。
    • No more passengers than working seat belts.
      车内人数不得超过安全带数量。

    2. Highways & Expressways 高速与快速路

    • G1 drivers are not allowed on:
      • 400-series highways (401, 403, 404, 407, 410, etc.)
      • Expressways: Gardiner Expressway, Don Valley Parkway (DVP), Queen Elizabeth Way (QEW).
        G1 驾驶员禁止在以下道路行驶：400 系列高速（如 401、403、404、407、410 等）以及快速路（嘉迪纳高速、唐谷公园道、伊丽莎白女王道）。

    3. Speed Limits 限速

    • In cities/towns: 50 km/h unless posted otherwise.
      市区：限速 50 公里/小时，除非有特殊标志。
    • On rural highways: 80 km/h unless posted otherwise.
      乡村公路：限速 80 公里/小时，除非有特殊标志。
    • On 400-series highways: usually 100 km/h.
      400 系列高速：一般限速 100 公里/小时。

    4. Right of Way 行车优先权

    • At uncontrolled intersections: the vehicle on the right goes first.
      无信号路口：右侧来车先行。
    • Pedestrians always have right of way at crosswalks.
      行人在人行横道上永远有优先权。
    • Emergency vehicles (with lights/sirens on): pull to the right, stop.
      遇到警车、救护车、消防车鸣笛：靠右停下。

    5. Traffic Lights 交通灯

    • Flashing red: Stop, then go when safe.
      红灯闪烁：停车，确认安全后通行。
    • Flashing green (Ontario): You may go left, right, or straight (others must stop).
      绿灯闪烁（安省特有）：可以左转、右转或直行，其他方向车辆必须停下。

    6. Parking 停车

    • Do not park within 3 meters of a fire hydrant.
      消防栓 3 米范围内禁止停车。
    • Do not park within 9 meters of an intersection with traffic lights.
      有交通灯的路口 9 米内禁止停车。

    📘 这份清单覆盖了 G1 笔试最常见的考点（限制、限速、优先权、信号灯、停车），基本背熟就能应付大部分题目。

    02

    How many multiple-choice questions are on the G1 knowledge test?

    The Ontario G1 knowledge test has:

    • 40 multiple-choice questions in total ✅
      • 20 questions on road signs
      • 20 questions on rules of the road

    To pass, you must get at least 16/20 correct in each section (signs and rules).

    📘 中文解释：
    安省 G1 笔试一共 40 道选择题：

    • 20 道是 交通标志
    • 20 道是 道路规则

    及格标准是：两个部分分别至少答对 16 题。

    03

    Which licence class must you hold before attempting to obtain Class D, A, C, E, F, or B licences?

    You must first hold a Class G licence (full licence) before attempting to obtain a Class D, A, C, E, F, or B licence.

    📘 English Explanation:
    In Ontario, the Class G licence is the standard full driver’s licence for cars, vans, and small trucks. Before you can apply for commercial or larger vehicle licences — like Class A (tractor-trailers), Class D (large trucks), or bus licences (Class B, C, E, F) — you need to already have your full G licence. This ensures you have enough driving experience before handling larger or commercial vehicles.

    📘 中文解释：
    在安省，Class G 驾照 是标准的全牌（适用于普通小汽车、面包车、小卡车）。在申请更高级别的驾照（如 Class A 大货车、Class D 卡车、Class B/C/E/F 公交车等）之前，必须先取得 全牌 G 驾照。这样可以保证驾驶员先有充足的驾驶经验，再学习更复杂的大型或商用车辆。
