# Chapter 15

So then the seventh planet was the Earth.  
那么，第七颗行星就是地球了。

The Earth is not just an ordinary planet!  
地球可不是一颗普通的行星！

One can count: there are 111 kings (not forgetting, to be sure, the Negro kings among them), 7,000 geographers, 900,000 businessmen, 7,500,000 tipplers, and 311,000,000 conceited men—that is to say, about 2,000,000,000 grown-ups.  
数一数：地球上有一百一十一个国王（当然，包括黑人国王），七千个地理学家，九十万个商人，七百五十万个酒鬼，三亿一千一百万个爱虚荣的人。也就是说，大约二十亿个大人。

To give you an idea of the size of the Earth, I will tell you that before the invention of electricity  
为了让你对地球的大小有个概念，我要告诉你：在电被发明之前——

It was necessary to maintain, over the whole of the six continents, a veritable army of 462,511 lamplighters for the street lamps.  
在整个六大洲上，为了点亮路灯，需要维持一支四十六万二千五百一十一人的庞大点灯人大军。

Seen from a slight distance, that would make a splendid spectacle.  
从稍远的地方望去，那真是一幅壮观的景象。

The movements of this army would be regulated like those of the ballet in the opera.  
这支大军的动作，就像歌剧院里的芭蕾舞一样整齐划一。

First would come the turn of the lamplighters of New Zealand and Australia.  
首先出场的是新西兰和澳大利亚的点灯人。

Having set their lamps alight, these would go off to sleep.  
点亮了灯之后，他们就回去睡觉了。

Next, the lamplighters of China and Siberia would enter for their steps in the dance, and then they too would be waved back into the wings.  
接着是中国和西伯利亚的点灯人上场，随后他们也退到幕布后去了。

After that would come the turn of the lamplighters of Russia and the Indies;  
然后轮到俄罗斯和印度的点灯人；

then those of Africa and Europe, then those of South America; then those of North America.  
接下来是非洲和欧洲的，然后是南美的，再然后是北美的。

And never would they make a mistake in the order of their entry upon the stage. It would be magnificent.  
他们从来不会弄错自己上场的次序。那真是壮丽无比。

Only the man who was in charge of the single lamp at the North Pole, and his colleague who was responsible for the single lamp at the South Pole—only these two would live free from toil and care: they would be busy twice a year.  
只有北极那位负责唯一一盏路灯的人，以及他在南极的同行，才过着无忧无虑的清闲生活：他们一年只忙两次。
