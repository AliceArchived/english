      1. Simply means something is of low quality or not satisfactory but doesn't necessarily mean it's completely useless.
      2. More general and neutral than "It's no good."
      3. Example:
         1. "This movie is not good." (It’s bad, but it still exists as a movie.)
         2. "The soup is not good; it's too salty." (It could still be eaten but isn’t tasty.)
