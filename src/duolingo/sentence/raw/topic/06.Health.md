# 6. Health

1. 记得把这个药存放在冰箱里。
   1. Remember to **keep** this medicine in the fridge.(~~put~~)
2. 记得一天吃三次药。
   1. Remember to take **the** medicine three times a day.
3. If you get sick, don't come to work.
   1. 如果你生病了，就不要来上班。
4. 我的表哥感冒了。
   1. My cousin **has** **a cold**. (~~got~~)
5. 他感冒了，但是他马上会感觉好一点的。
   1. He **has a cold**, but he will feel better **soon**.
6. 你应该吃一些感冒药。
   1. You should take some **cold** medicine.
7. 我的头一点也不痛。
   1. My head **doesn't** hurt at all.
8. 这会弄伤你的眼睛。
   1. This will **hurt** your eyes! ( ~~hurts~~)
9. 她下班后**去了一趟**医院。
   1. **She stopped at the hospital after work?**
10. 你跟那个医生预约的？
    1. Which doctor **did** you make an appointment **with**?
11. 孩子们应该多久去看一次牙医？
    1. How often should the children go to **the** dentist?
12. 我可以预约九点吗？
    1. Can I make an appointment **for nine**?
13. 你的胳膊能动吗？
    1. Can your arm move?
    2. Can you move your arm?
14. 不要去那里，你会摔倒！
    1. Don't go there, you **will fall down.**
15. 怀特医生今天没有空，但是她明天有空。 1. Dr. White isn't free today, but she has time tomorrow. 2. Dr. White isn't available today, but she's available tomorrow.
    My dentist is available tomorrow at 11 a.m.
16. 她跟王医生有一个预约。
    1. She **has** an appointment with Dr. Wang. (~~had~~)
17. 他应该什么时候预约？
    1. When should he **make an appointment**?
18. 1. She always hurt**s** her left arm.
19. 你的脖子怎么了？
    1. What's **wrong** with your neck?
    2. What **happened** to your neck?
20. 你的预约是在早上还是在下午？
    1. **Is** your appointment **in** the morning or **in** the afternoon. (~~Are~~ )
21. 他昨天去看了医生， 今天他感觉好一点。
    1. He went to the doctor yesterday, **and today he feels better**.
22. 他牙疼。
    1. He **has a toothache**. (~~get~~)
23. 爸爸不应该太晚吃他的药。
    1. Dad shouldn't take his **medicine** too late.
24. 你生病了吗？
25. Did you get sick?
26. 你感觉还好吗，你怎么了？
    1. How do you feel like? What's wrong with you?
    2. Do you feel OK, what's wrong with you?
27. 我总是问牙医关于我牙齿的问题。
    1. I always ask the dentist questions about my teeth.
28. Keep this medicine **in** the fridge.
29. I have a cold and I need some medicine.
30. 他没事。
    1. He's all right.
31. If I use the phone for too long, my neck hurts.
32. I had a **headache** yesterday.
33. I **have a cold** and I need some **medicine**.
34. He'll call **the dentist's office** after work.
