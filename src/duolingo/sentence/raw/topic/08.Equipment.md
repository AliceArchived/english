# 8. Equipment

## 1. Computer

1. 我的笔记本电脑没电了。
   1. My laptop is **dead**.
2. Can you share your **screen**?
3. 你电脑的电池怎么了？
   1. What happened to your computer battery?
4. 你的笔记本电脑出了问题吗？
   1. Does your laptop meet any problems?
   2. **Is there** a problem with your laptop?
5. Oh no, my battery is dead.
6. Is your battery is dead.
7. I'll share my screen in a moment.
8. I can't see you **very well**.
9. I can't hear you very well.
10. This website is very slow.
11. I'm sorry, I can't hear you very well.


## 2. Internet

1. Sorry, my internet is **very slow**.
2. She'll be **online** in a moment.
3. 我的孩子们将会在一点半上网课。
   1. My kids will **be** taking an online class **at** half past one.
4. 孩子们从来不保持安静，当我在开一个在线会议时。
   1. The kids never keep quiet when I'm in an **online meeting**.
5. 他在跟他的朋友在网上聊天。
   1. He's **chatting online** with his friend.
6. 在这个网站上有太多信息。
   1. There is **too much** information **on** this website.
