# 10. Art

## 1. General

1. 昨晚到演出很糟糕。
   1. The **show** was **terrible** last night. (~~play~~)
   2. “**play**” is **too specific** and may not match all types of performances; “show” is a broader and more natural word in casual conversation. Also, “terrible” is stronger and more expressive than “very bad”, which better suits spoken English at Band 7+ level.
   3. **“play”** 指的是一种特定类型的表演，通常是：
      - **一部戏剧**（比如莎士比亚的戏、话剧、舞台剧）
      - 在剧院（theater）里由演员**表演剧本台词**的节目
      - We saw a **play** by Shakespeare.（我们看了一部莎士比亚的戏剧）
      - The school is putting on a **play** about World War II.（学校在演出一部关于二战的戏剧）
   4. 而 **“show”** 是更广义的词，可以指：
      - 戏剧（play）
      - 音乐会（concert）
      - 舞蹈表演（dance performance）
      - 脱口秀、综艺、电视节目（TV shows, talent shows）
2. 我上周末在爵士酒吧玩得很尽兴。
   1. I had a great time at the **jazz bar** last weekend.
3. You'll be a **fantastic** dancer!
4. Our **seats** **at the concert** were not bad.
5. This stadium **is famous for** its size.
6. That cat is famous for the rock songs she sings.
7. That rap star is famous for the clothes that he wears.
8. 他是乐队里唯一的男人。
   1. He was the only man who was **in the band**.
9. 她是乐队里唯一的歌手。
   1. She was the only singer who was in the band.
10. She was the only person who was sleeping **at** the concert.
11. My cat is an **artist**.
12. What's he famous for?
13. 这个艺术家以什么画而闻名？
    1. What painting is this artist famous for?
14. **谁是队里最糟糕的舞者？**
    1. Who is the worst dancer in the team?
    2. Who is the worst dancer **on** the team?

## 2. Museum

1. What's this **museum** famous for?
2. The museum looks beautiful **in the sun**.
3. Are we at the front of the **museum**?
4. The front of the museum is very interesting.
5. Wow, this is **such a** beautiful museum.
6. The museum is open every day, except holidays.
7. 除了周六以外，博物馆每天都开门到下午六点。
   1. **Every day except Saturday**, the museum **is open** until 6 p.m.
8. 博物馆的正面看起来很漂亮。
   1. **The front of** the museum looks pretty.

## 3. Painting

1. This is a beautiful painting.
2. Do you enjoy **painting**?
3. Does that painting look different **in the sun**?
4. That artist loves to paint outside **in the sun**.

## 4. Music

1. 她是一个很棒的吉他手，但是她是最糟糕的歌手。
   1. She's a **fantastic guitar player**, but she's the worst singer.
2. My cat loves **rock**.
3. That song was **not bad**, but I don't want to listen to it again.
4. He was singing **pop songs** all night.
5. My grandpa listen to **rap** sometimes.
6. 你最喜欢的是那个，流行，摇滚还是说唱。
   1. Which is your favorite, **pop, rock, or rap**?
7. We listened to a song yesterday that was really cool.
8. You're not the worst singer, I'm **worse** than you!
9. 爵士乐是我最喜欢的音乐类型。
   1. **Jazz** is my **favorite** kind of music!
10. 我们都喜欢这个摇滚明星。
    1. We both like this rock star.
    2. We **all** like this rock star.
11. 这首歌在英国非常受欢迎.
    1. This song is very popular in **the** UK. (**the United Kingdom**)
    2. 需要加 **“the”** 的国家有：
       - the UK (the United Kingdom)
       - the USA (the United States of America)
       - the Netherlands
    3. 不需要加 “the” 的国家(单一地名，不带“王国、共和国”等结构)：
       - China
       - Canada
       - Japan
       - Brazil
12. 我的女朋友不喜欢听说唱。
    1. My girlfriend **doesn't** like **listening** **to** rap.
13. 那首歌还不错，但是我不想再听一遍。
    1. That song **was** not bad, but I don't want to listen **to** it again.
14. 如果她多练习的话，我的猫可以把舞跳得更好。
    1. If she practices more, my cat **can** get better at dancing.
15. 我上周末在爵士酒吧玩得很尽兴。
    1. I had a great time at the jazz bar last weekend.
16. 昨晚的音乐会还不错。
    1. The concert **was not bad** last night.
17. 你喜欢歌剧吗？
    1. Do you like **opera**?
18. If it rains this weekend, we'll **see** an opera **at** the theater.
19. Do you **listen** to **jazz**?
20. You can hear jazz in **Central Park** every summer.
21. 如果你有空，我们可以买明天的歌剧票。
    1. If you've free, we can **get tickets to the opera** tomorrow.
22. Let's **see an opera** at the **theater**.
23. Do you want to go **to** an opera together?
24. 你喜欢昨晚的戏剧吗？
    1. Did you like the play last night?
25. I want to see a **play** in New York.
26. If you like, we can see a play together.
