# Stuck in the Closet - B3

Eddy and Oscar are stuck in the school gym's **supply closet**. The door won't open.

- **Oscar**: This is unbelievable. I'd rather be anywhere else than stuck here.
- **Eddy**: I think it's nice! It's like a little vacation!
- **Oscar**: A vacation?! This closet smells like old socks!
- **Eddy**: Hey, speaking of socks! Did I **ever** tell you about my lucky socks? I wore them in my college football games —
- **Oscar**: Eddy, please, I don't want to hear —
- **Eddy**: So anyway, we were playing the biggest game of the year. We **were losing**, but then —
- **Oscar**: This is my nightmare.
- **Eddy**: — I scored the winning goal with those socks! Amazing, right?
- **Oscar**: Eddy, can we please focus on getting out of here?
- **Eddy**: Sure, but first, do you want to hear about Junior's **science fair project**? It involved a volcano —
- **Oscar**: Universe! Please make him stop talking! I'll do anything!

The door stays shut. Eddy keeps talking.

- **Eddy**: Oh! And then there was the time I babysat my nieces. We made pancakes, **but instead of flour** —
- **Oscar**: Fine! I'll let you choose the next school play! Just stop talking!

Suddenly, the **janitor** opens the door.

- **Eddy**: Cool! Thanks, Oscar. I already have ideas for the play.
- **Oscar**: Wait, ideas?
- **Eddy**: Yeah! I want all the kids to wear penguin costumes and sing in a giant noodle bowl.
- **Oscar**: … **What on earth have I done**?

  - n. 守卫；门警；幽门；看门人

被困在储藏室

Eddy 和 Oscar 被困在学校体育馆的储藏室里。门打不开。

- **Oscar**: 难以置信。我宁愿在任何地方，也不想被困在这里。

- **Eddy**: 嘿，说到袜子！我有没有告诉过你我的幸运袜子？我在大学橄榄球比赛时都穿它们——
- **Oscar**: Eddy，拜托，我不想听——
- **Eddy**: 总之，那是年度最大的一场比赛。我们本来要输了，但后来——
- **Oscar**: 这简直是我的噩梦。

门依然紧闭。Eddy 继续说个不停。

- **Eddy**: 哦！还有一次我帮我侄女们看孩子。我们做煎饼，但没有用面粉——

突然，管理员打开了门。

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-30

**Why does Oscar let Eddy choose the school play?**

Well, Oscar **finally** lets Eddy choose the next school play **simply because** he can’t stand the endless talking anymore. They **are stuck together** in the closet, and instead of helping to solve the problem, Eddy just **keeps going on and on** with his silly stories about socks, science projects, and pancakes. Oscar **feels trapped** not only **physically** but also **mentally**, so to make Eddy stop, he **makes a deal** and lets him take charge of the play.

嗯，Oscar 最后让 Eddy 选择下一个学校的剧目，纯粹是因为他再也受不了那没完没了的唠叨了。他们一起被困在储物间里，而 Eddy 不但没有帮忙解决问题，反而一直不停地讲一些关于袜子、科学展和煎饼的无聊故事。Oscar 感到不仅身体被困住了，连精神也快要崩溃了，所以为了让 Eddy 闭嘴，他只好妥协，让他负责剧目的选择。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ because it:

- Expands the answer into **a coherent narrative**.
- Uses **natural spoken connectors** like “well,” “and instead of,” “so.”
- Shows **emotional engagement** with words like “can’t stand,” “trapped,” “deal.”
- Clear, fluent, and demonstrates paraphrasing of the original text.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-30

**Why does Oscar let Eddy choose the school play?**

Oscar allows Eddy to choose the next school play because he **desperately** wants him to stop talking. Both of them are trapped in **the supply closet**, but rather than focusing on how to get out, Eddy continuously tells **irrelevant stories** about his socks, a science project, and even pancakes. This endless chatter becomes **unbearable** for Oscar, who feels **frustrated** and **helpless** in **such a confined space**. As a result, he **compromises** by giving Eddy **the privilege of** deciding the play, simply as a way to **regain** some peace and silence.

Oscar 允许 Eddy 选择下一个学校的剧目，是因为他非常渴望让 Eddy 停止说话。他们两人被困在储物间里，但 Eddy 并没有想着如何逃出去，而是不断讲一些无关紧要的故事，比如袜子、科学展甚至煎饼。这种没完没了的唠叨让 Oscar 难以忍受，他在狭小的空间里感到极度沮丧和无助。因此，他只能妥协，把选择剧目的权利交给 Eddy，只是为了重新获得一些安宁与安静。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ because it:

- Contains more than 100 words with **logical flow**.
- Uses **academic-style vocabulary** such as “desperately,” “irrelevant,” “unbearable,” “compromises.”
- Develops the idea with **cause–effect structure** (“because… but… as a result…”).
- Demonstrates clarity, coherence, and lexical resource expected at Band 7+.

### Speaking vs Writing Feature Comparison

#### 2025-08-30

| Feature                 | Speaking Version                                                                 | Writing Version                                                            |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sentence length         | Short to medium, natural pauses (e.g., “so to make Eddy stop, he makes a deal…”) | Longer, more complex sentences with subordinate clauses                    |
| Tone                    | Conversational, emotional (“can’t stand,” “silly stories”)                       | Formal, objective, analytical (“irrelevant stories,” “unbearable chatter”) |
| Common connectors       | “Well,” “and instead of,” “so”                                                   | “because,” “rather than,” “as a result,” “therefore”                       |
| Vocabulary choice       | Everyday, spoken style (“deal,” “trapped,” “keeps going on”)                     | More formal, precise (“compromises,” “desperately,” “privilege”)           |
| Emotional expression    | Strongly expressed annoyance, frustration in casual words                        | Controlled, indirect description of frustration                            |
| Rhetorical/detail style | Storytelling with examples and exaggeration                                      | Logical cause–effect explanation, balanced detail                          |

### Practice History

**Why does Oscar let Eddy choose the school play?**

Helpful Phrases: Oscar lets/ stop talking/ stuck in

Oscar lets Eddy choose the next school play to trade him stop talking. Because they both stuck in a closet and Eddy continues talking.

> Corrections:

1. Fixed grammar: “to trade him stop talking” → “to make him stop talking.”
2. Also corrected tense/structure:
   1. “they both stuck” → “they are both stuck,”
   2. “Eddy continues talking” → “Eddy keeps talking.”

Oscar lets Eddy choose the next school play to **make** him stop talking, because they **are** both stuck in a closet and Eddy **keeps** talking.

Oscar 让 Eddy 选择下一个学校的剧目，是为了让他闭嘴，因为他们被一起困在储物间里，而 Eddy 一直在喋喋不休。
