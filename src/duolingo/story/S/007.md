# Smelling Salts, Bottle, Cup - B3

Lin, Joined 2009
Items Sold: 524

Hi there! I was cleaning up my apartment (well, my grandma Lucy's apartment), and I found some interesting things. They might sound surprising, but they could be useful to you. This is what I have today:

**Smelling Salts - Free**

I found these smelling salts in a drawer. They might belong to my grandmother or maybe a long-lost hero. Who knows? They're not expired, so they're good to go. If you feel faint or need **a wake-up call**, these are perfect for you. They're free!

**Glass Bottle - 3 dollars**

This glass bottle once held some mysterious drink. Now it's empty, clean, and waiting for its next role. Fill it with water, sand from the beach, or even some homemade sauce. It's like a blank canvas for glass!

**Empty Cup - 1 dollar**

I have an empty cup that didn't make it to the recycling bin. It's perfectly fine, really. Use it for your morning coffee or a cup of noodles. It's sturdy and ready to be filled with whatever your heart desires.

That's all I have for today!

Call, text, or email me!

## Vocabulary

- sturdy: 英 /'stɜːdi/, 美 /'stɜːrdi/
  - adj. 强健的；坚定的；健全的
  - n. [医]羊晕倒病
- wake-up call
  - “wake-up call” 的确是“提神”的意思，但要注意它的语境。
  - 在广告或物品介绍里，如果是和嗅盐（smelling salts）搭配，就表示让人清醒、恢复精神的东西，可以译作“提神”或“让你清醒过来”。
  - 在日常英语里，“wake-up call” 还可以是比喻用法，指“警醒/提醒”，比如某件事让你意识到需要改变。
- They're not **expired**, so they're good to go.
  - expired: ended

## Chinese

Lin，2009 年加入

**嗅盐 - 免费**

我在抽屉里发现了这些嗅盐。它们可能属于我奶奶，也可能属于某个失落已久的英雄。谁知道呢？它们还没过期，所以可以使用。如果你感到头晕，或者需要提神，这些正适合你。免费送！

**玻璃瓶 - 3 美元**

这个玻璃瓶曾经装过一些神秘的饮料。现在它是空的、干净的，正等待着下一次使命。你可以装水、海滩上的沙子，甚至一些自制酱料。对于玻璃来说，它就像一块空白画布！

**空杯子 - 1 美元**

我有一个没进回收桶的空杯子。它状态很好，真的。你可以用它喝早晨的咖啡，或者泡一杯方便面。它很结实，随时可以装下你心之所想的任何东西。

今天就这些！

打电话、发短信或发邮件联系我吧！

## Sentences

- I have an empty cup that didn't **make it** to the recycling bin.
  - Here the tone is casual and slightly humorous:
    - Literally: the cup never ended up in the recycling bin.
    - Implied meaning: instead of being thrown away/recycled, it’s still here and available for someone to use.
  - It’s like saying, “It was supposed to go to recycling, but it didn’t, so now I’m offering it to you.”

## Speaking

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**Which item seems most useful? Why?**

I think the glass bottle seems to be the most useful item — and the **higher price** also suggests it's **of some value**. If I had to choose something, I’d go with the bottle; otherwise, I wouldn’t take any of them.

我认为玻璃瓶看起来是最有用的物品 — 它较高的价格也说明它可能更有价值。如果我非选一样的话，我会选那个瓶子；否则，我一样都不会选。

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**Which item seems most useful? Why?**

Personally, I think the empty cup would be the most useful item. If I had to choose, I’d definitely go for it because it’s **practical** and can be used every single day — whether for coffee, tea, or even **instant noodles**. The smelling salts are interesting, but I’m not sure I’d ever need them, and the glass bottle feels a bit overpriced for what it does. A simple cup, on the other hand, is affordable, reliable, and always comes in handy.

我个人认为空杯子是最有用的物品。如果必须选择，我肯定会选它，因为它实用，可以每天使用——无论是喝咖啡、茶，还是泡方便面。嗅盐很有趣，但我不确定自己会用到，而玻璃瓶相对于它的用途来说有点贵。相比之下，一个简单的杯子价格实惠、可靠，而且总能派上用场。

> Explanation:

This version reaches Band 7+ for Speaking because it uses a clear opinion with justification, natural conversational connectors (“personally,” “on the other hand”), varied sentence structures, and concrete examples. It sounds fluent and coherent, with a logical flow from preference to reasons.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

Among the three items, the empty cup appears to be the most useful. If I were given the choice, I would select it because it serves a wide range of purposes in daily life, from holding coffee or tea to preparing instant noodles. In contrast, the smelling salts, while somewhat **intriguing**, would have **limited relevance** in my routine, as I rarely encounter situations that require them. The glass bottle, although **versatile**, is slightly overpriced **relative to** its function. Therefore, the empty cup stands out as a practical, cost-effective option that I could use regularly and without difficulty.

在这三件物品中，空杯子似乎是最有用的。如果让我选择，我会选它，因为它在日常生活中用途广泛，从装咖啡、茶到泡方便面都可以。相比之下，嗅盐虽然有趣，但在我的生活中几乎用不上。玻璃瓶虽然多功能，但相对于它的作用来说价格偏高。因此，空杯子作为一个实用且性价比高的选择，更能让我经常、轻松地使用。

> Explanation:

This version fits IELTS Writing Band 7+ because it uses formal tone, precise vocabulary (“serves a wide range of purposes,” “slightly overpriced relative to its function”), and clear paragraph organization. The reasoning is developed logically with contrast and conclusion, avoiding informal expressions.

### Practice History

**Which item seems most useful? Why?**

Helpful Phrases: I think/ The most useful is/ I would choose

I think the empty cup might be the most useful item. If I have to choose, I would choose the empty cup. Smelling salts, why **are they** smelling? Glass bottle is **okay**, but it's expensive **compared** to **its** funtion.

> Corrections:

1. Changed “If I have to choose” → “If I had to choose” (conditional mood for hypothetical situation).
2. Added quotation marks around “smelling” for clarity when questioning the word.
3. “Glass bottle is okay” → “The glass bottle is okay” (needs article).
4. Minor wording fix for “its funtion” → “its function” (spelling).

I think the empty cup might be the most useful item. If I **had** to choose, I would choose the empty cup. Smelling salts — why are they called “smelling”? **The** glass bottle is okay, but it's expensive compared to its function.

我认为空杯子可能是最有用的物品。如果必须选择，我会选择空杯子。嗅盐——为什么叫“嗅”盐呢？玻璃瓶还可以，但与它的功能相比，它的价格有点贵。

---

…but it's expensive compared to their function.
这里的 "compared to" 是 过去分词短语（非谓语），表示“与……相比”这种状语用法。

- 逻辑主语 是 “it”（bottle）
- 可以理解为省略了 "when compared to" 或 "if you compare them to"。

如果用 "compare"
必须改写句子结构，让 "compare" 成为谓语的一部分：

If you compare its price to its function, you'll find it's expensive.

> Corrections:

1. “and its price also suggests it” → 更自然表达为 “and its price suggests that as well”
2. “if I must choose one” → 更标准为虚拟语气 “if I had to pick one”

I think the most useful item is the glass bottle, and its price suggests that as well. I would choose the glass bottle if I **had** to pick one — otherwise, I wouldn't choose anything.

我觉得最有用的物品是那个玻璃瓶，从它的价格也可以看出这一点。如果我必须选一个，我会选它；如果不必选，我一个都不会要。
