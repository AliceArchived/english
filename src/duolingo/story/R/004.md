##### professional and personal settings

"professional and personal settings" 这个表达，尤其是在 **正式写作**（学术、工作场合）里。

- **professional settings** = 工作环境、职场场景（meetings, office, business context）
- **personal settings** = 私人环境、个人生活场景（family, friends, social life）

📌 举例：

- _“This skill is valuable in both professional and personal settings.”_  
  这句话很常见，意思是“这种技能在职场和个人生活中都很有用”。
- 在 IELTS Writing 或 academic essay 中，这种搭配显得很 **自然 + 正式 + 简洁**。

不过：

- 在 **口语 (Speaking)** 中，native speakers 可能更常用 **“at work and in daily life”** 或 **“in my job and in my personal life”**，因为说起来更口语化，更容易理解。

👉 所以建议：

- **Writing**：用 _“professional and personal settings”_（加分，学术感强）
- **Speaking**：用 _“at work and in my personal life”_（更自然，贴近口语）

### Speaking vs Writing Feature Comparison

#### 2025-09-04

| Feature                     | Speaking Version                                     | Writing Version                                                |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sentence length**         | Short to medium (10–20 words), easier to speak aloud | Longer, complex (20–30+ words), formal structures              |
| **Tone**                    | Conversational, personal (“Personally, I find…”)     | Academic, objective (“It not only… but also demonstrates…”)    |
| **Common connectors**       | “Among all the tips,” “Instead of,” “Personally”     | “However,” “Not only… but also,” “As it helps”                 |
| **Vocabulary choice**       | Everyday words: “practical,” “unkind,” “lighter”     | Formal/academic words: “conflict,” “emotional intelligence”    |
| **Emotional expression**    | Moderate, personal reflection about feelings         | Minimal, more focus on logic and argument                      |
| **Rhetorical/detail style** | Uses personal examples, feelings, soft expressions   | Uses logical reasoning, structured benefits, professional tone |
