# Locked in the Janitor's Office - B3

Oscar and Eddy are in the school janitor's office. Eddy is inspecting a mop while Oscar looks annoyed.

- **Eddy**: Wow, this mop is so shiny! You think the **janitor** works out? Good arms for mopping?
- **Oscar**: Eddy, stop. We shouldn't even be in here. Let's go.

Oscar tries the door, but it doesn't open.

- **Oscar**: Great. We've gotten ourselves locked in.
- **Eddy**: Oh! This is like an adventure!
- **Oscar**: No, Eddy. This is a nightmare.
- **Eddy**: Come on, Oscar. Let's make the best of it! I'll tell you about my workout routine.
- **Oscar**: No offense, Eddy, but I'd rather not.

Eddy doesn't stop.

- **Eddy**: So, Monday is arm day. You wouldn't believe how heavy Junior's toy box is. I lift it like 10 times!

Oscar glares at the window.

- **Oscar**: To make matters worse, there's no fresh air. I'm opening this window!

Oscar pulls at the window **latch**.

- **Eddy**: Careful not to break it —

The latch **snaps**.

- **Oscar**: Oh no…

Oscar tries to fix it, but the window is jammed — and the room now feels hotter.

- **Eddy**: Well, that didn't help. But hey, now we'll have more time to **bond**!
- **Oscar**: Bonding with you feels like being in a car accident.
- **Eddy**: Ouch, Oscar. That's not very nice. But you'll see, by the end of this, we'll be best friends!
- **Oscar**: Hilarious. Truly.

## Vocabulary

- janitor: 英 /'dʒænɪtə(r)/, 美 /'dʒænɪtər/
  - n. 守卫；门警；幽门；看门人
- latch: 英 /lætʃ/, 美 /lætʃ/
  - n. (门窗的)门闩, 碰锁
  - vt. & vi. 用碰锁锁上(门等)；闩上(门等)
    - 纠缠，缠住(某人)
    - 附着(在某物上)
    - 对…产生兴趣(并加以利用)
    - 理解，懂得，领会(想法或某人的话等)

## Chinese

被锁在管理员办公室

Oscar 和 Eddy 在学校的管理员办公室。Eddy 正在检查一把拖把，而 Oscar 看起来很烦躁。

- **Eddy**: 哇，这拖把好亮！你觉得这个**管理员**是不是经常锻炼？拖地的手臂力量真不错吧？
- **Oscar**: Eddy，停下。我们根本就不该在这里。走吧。

Oscar 试了试门，但门打不开。

- **Oscar**: 太好了。我们把自己锁在里面了。
- **Eddy**: 哦！这就像一次冒险！
- **Oscar**: 不，Eddy。这是场噩梦。
- **Eddy**: 来吧，Oscar。让我们尽量利用这个机会！我来告诉你我的锻炼计划。
- **Oscar**: 无意冒犯，Eddy，但我宁愿不要听。

Eddy 并没有停下。

- **Eddy**: 所以，星期一是练臂力的日子。你不会相信 Junior 的玩具箱有多重。我能举起来大概 10 次！

Oscar 瞪着窗户。

- **Oscar**: 更糟的是，这里没有新鲜空气。我来开这个窗户！

Oscar 拉住窗户的**插销**。

- **Eddy**: 小心别弄坏了——

插销**啪**的一声断了。

- **Oscar**: 哦，不……

Oscar 试着修复它，但窗户卡住了——而且房间感觉更闷热了。

- **Eddy**: 好吧，这没帮上忙。但嘿，现在我们有更多的时间来增进感情了！
- **Oscar**: 和你增进感情就像遭遇一场车祸。
- **Eddy**: 好痛啊，Oscar。这可不太友好。但你会看到的，到最后，我们会成为最好的朋友！
- **Oscar**: 真好笑啊。真的。

## Sentences

- The latch **snaps**.
  - 插销**啪**的一声断了。
  - 英文里的 snap 在这里是动词，表示 “突然断裂 / 发出清脆的响声”。
  - 当我们写 The latch snaps. 时，意思是“插销突然断掉，并伴随清脆的声响”。

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-26

**Why did the room feel hotter?**

Well, the room felt hotter mainly because Oscar couldn’t **get any fresh air**. At first, the door wouldn’t open, so they were already stuck. Then, when Oscar tried to open the window, **the latch snapped** and **the window got jammed**. That **meant** no air could come in. **On top of that**, Eddy just kept talking **nonstop**, which annoyed Oscar even more. So, between the broken window and Eddy’s endless chatter, the room felt extremely hot and uncomfortable.

房间感觉更热，主要是因为 Oscar 没法呼吸到新鲜空气。一开始门打不开，他们就已经被困住了。后来 Oscar 试着开窗，结果卡扣断了，窗户卡住了，空气进不来。更糟的是，Eddy 一直不停地说话，让 Oscar 更烦躁。所以，加上坏掉的窗户和 Eddy 没完没了的唠叨，房间就感觉格外闷热、不舒服。

> Explanation:

This answer is Band 7+ for Speaking because:

- Clear, fluent storytelling in past tense.
- Uses natural spoken connectors (_“at first”_, _“then”_, _“on top of that”_, _“so”_).
- **Paraphrases key points** instead of memorized phrasing.
- Adds **mild emotional language** (_“nonstop”_, _“extremely hot and uncomfortable”_), which makes it sound natural.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-26

**Why did the room feel hotter?**
The room felt hotter because Oscar and Eddy were **completely trapped** **without access to fresh air**. First of all, the door would not open, which meant they could not leave the janitor’s office. In an attempt to **improve** the **situation**, Oscar tried to open the window, but the latch snapped, leaving the window jammed. As a result, no fresh air could **circulate inside**. What made matters worse was Eddy’s constant talking, which **increased** Oscar’s **irritation** and created a sense of discomfort. Therefore, the broken window **combined with** Eddy’s chatter made the room feel unbearably hot.

房间感觉更热，是因为 Oscar 和 Eddy 完全被困住，无法呼吸到新鲜空气。首先，门打不开，这意味着他们不能离开管理员的办公室。为了改善情况，Oscar 试着打开窗户，但卡扣断了，窗户卡住了。结果，房间里没有空气可以流通。更糟的是，Eddy 不停地说话，这增加了 Oscar 的烦躁感，也让他觉得更不舒服。因此，坏掉的窗户和 Eddy 的唠叨结合在一起，让房间感觉热得难以忍受。

> Explanation:

This answer is Band 7+ for Writing because:

- Clear logical structure (_first of all_, **_in an attempt_**, _as a result_, _what made matters worse_, _therefore_).
- Uses formal and precise vocabulary (_“access to fresh air”_, _“circulate”_, _“unbearably hot”_).
- Sentences are complex with subordinate clauses.
- Concise but fully developed explanation.
