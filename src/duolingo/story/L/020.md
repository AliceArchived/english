# Lost Keys Mystery - B3

Eddy's Advice Column

Hello, friends!

I'm Eddy. Today, the question is, "What do you do when you lose your keys and **start with the wild accusations**?" Well, I am no detective, but I do have a secret talent for losing keys. Here's what you can do:

**Stay Calm and Think It Through**

Before you **start throwing accusations around** like a soccer ball, take a deep breath. Let me remind you, wild accusations have victims! It's a good idea to think it through. The keys might just be in your other pants pocket. I once found mine inside a box of cereal! Who knew keys could hide so well?

**Ask for Alibis**

If you still can't find your keys, ask everyone around if they have an **alibi**. Your son says he was playing outside? Ask if they can tell you what game! Your neighbor says they were at a car wash? Ask what time! If they have solid alibis, **move on**. Sometimes, nobody has stolen anything.

**Get Organized**

Finally, make sure you get organized. Keep your keys in the same **spot**. I have a pot by the door where I drop my keys. If you remember to use it, you'll always know where your keys are. And hey, no more wild accusations, right?

With these tips, you'll solve the mystery of the missing keys in no time!

Stay strong!  
Eddy

## Chinese

丢失钥匙之谜

Eddy 的建议专栏

你好，朋友们！

我是 Eddy。今天的问题是：“当你丢了钥匙并**开始胡乱指责时**，你该怎么办？”嗯，我可不是侦探，但我确实有一个丢钥匙的秘密天赋。以下是你可以做的事：

**保持冷静并认真思考**

在你像踢足球一样到处乱甩指责之前，先深呼吸一下。让我提醒你，胡乱指责可是会伤害到别人的！仔细想一想是个好主意。钥匙可能就在你的另一条裤子口袋里。我曾经在一盒麦片里找到过我的钥匙！谁能想到钥匙能藏得这么好？

**询问不在场证明**

如果你还是找不到钥匙，就问问周围的人是否有不在场证明。你儿子说他在外面玩？问问他能不能告诉你玩了什么游戏！你的邻居说他们去洗车了？问问几点钟！如果他们有可靠的不在场证明，就继续找。 有时候，根本没人偷东西。

**保持有序**

最后，确保你保持有序。把钥匙放在同一个地方。我在门口放了一个小罐子，用来丢钥匙。如果你记得用它，你就总能知道钥匙在哪里。嘿，这样就不会再有胡乱指责了，对吧？

有了这些小技巧，你很快就能解开丢失钥匙的谜团！

保持坚强！  
Eddy

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-09-04

**What part of Eddy's advice is most helpful?**

In my opinion, the most helpful part of Eddy’s advice is his suggestion to keep the keys in the same spot. While the other tips are useful, they mainly **address the problem** after it occurs. **By contrast**, being organized is **a preventive strategy**, which means the problem can be avoided in the first place. For example, if I consistently place my keys in a **designated** bowl near the entrance, I would **eliminate unnecessary stress** and **avoid making false accusations**. This small habit not only saves time but also builds a sense of order in daily life.

在我看来，Eddy 的建议中最有帮助的部分是他提出要把钥匙放在同一个地方。虽然其他提示也有用，但它们主要是在问题发生之后才去解决。相比之下，保持有条理是一种预防性的策略，可以从一开始就避免问题。例如，如果我把钥匙固定放在门口的一个小碗里，就能避免不必要的压力，也不会产生错误的指责。这个小习惯不仅节省时间，还能在日常生活中建立一种秩序感。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ because:

- Academic tone with structured reasoning (“while,” “by contrast,” “for example”).
- Variety of complex structures: conditional clause, contrastive linking.
- Rich vocabulary: “preventive strategy,” “eliminate unnecessary stress,” “designated.”
- Logical progression — clear argument supported with example.
