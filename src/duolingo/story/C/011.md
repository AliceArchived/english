# Cleaning the Park - B3

Bea and Lin are at the city park early in the morning. Bea holds a **clipboard** and looks excited. Lin is sitting on a bench, drinking coffee.

- **Bea**: Lin, today we are setting a record! The most trash collected in one park in one day.
- **Lin**: Sounds like a lot of **bending and lifting**. I'm not really a 'trash-picking' kind of person.
- **Bea**: Lin! This is for the environment! We need to clean the park and beat the record.
- **Lin**: Maybe we can just tell people we tried?
- **Bea**: No! We need to actually do it. I even made a schedule. Come on, get started!

Bea **races off** to start picking up trash. Lin stays on the bench, watching runners jog by.

- **Bea**: Lin! Are you seriously just sitting there? I can't do this alone!
- **Lin**: Don't worry, Bea. I have an idea.

Bea shakes her head and keeps working, getting annoyed as Lin stays on the bench. Suddenly, a group of runners gathers in the park near Lin.

- **Bea**: What is going on over there?

Bea walks over and sees the runners picking up trash as they jog.

- **Bea**: Lin! What is happening?
- **Lin**: Oh, I told them this was part of **an extreme race**. I said they'd win if they collected the most trash.
- **Bea**: You lied?
- **Lin**: No, I just… didn't mention there's no prize.

In under an hour, the park is spotless, and the runners cheer.

- **Bea**: Lin, I can't believe this actually worked. We broke the record!
- **Lin**: Great. Now, can I go back to sitting on the bench?

## Chinese

清理公园

清晨，Bea 和 Lin 在市公园里。Bea 拿着写字板，显得很兴奋。Lin 坐在长椅上喝咖啡。

- **Bea**：Lin，今天我们要创造纪录！一天之内在一个公园里捡到最多的垃圾。
- **Lin**：听起来要弯腰搬东西很多次。我可不是那种“捡垃圾”的人。
- **Bea**：Lin！这是为了环境！我们必须清理公园并打破纪录。
- **Lin**：也许我们可以就告诉别人我们努力过了？
- **Bea**：不行！我们得真的去做。我甚至还做了时间表。来吧，开始行动！

Bea 冲出去开始捡垃圾。Lin 仍然坐在长椅上，看着跑步的人经过。

- **Bea**：Lin！你真的就这么坐着吗？我一个人可干不了！
- **Lin**：别担心，Bea。我有个主意。

Bea 摇摇头继续干活，Lin 还坐在长椅上，这让她有点恼火。突然，一群跑者聚集在 Lin 附近的公园里。

- **Bea**：那边发生什么事了？

Bea 走过去，看到跑者们一边跑一边捡垃圾。

- **Bea**：Lin！这是怎么回事？
- **Lin**：哦，我告诉他们这是极限赛的一部分。我说谁捡的垃圾最多就会赢。
- **Bea**：你撒谎了？
- **Lin**：没有啊，我只是……没提到没有奖品。

不到一个小时，公园变得干干净净，跑者们欢呼起来。

- **Bea**：Lin，我简直不敢相信这真的成功了。我们打破了纪录！
- **Lin**：太好了。现在，我能回去坐在长椅上了吗？

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-20

**What did Lin tell the runners?**

Lin told the runners that the activity was actually part of a race where they had to pick up trash, and the person who collected the most would be **considered** the winner. She made it sound **competitive and exciting**, which really **motivated** the group to join in. The funny part, though, is that she **left out** the fact that there was no actual prize. So, in a clever way, she encouraged them to clean the park without even realizing it.

Lin 告诉跑步的人，这个活动其实是一场比赛，他们需要捡垃圾，而谁捡得最多就会被视为赢家。她把这件事说得很有竞争性和趣味性，这确实激励了大家参与进来。不过有趣的是，她没有提到实际上根本没有奖品。所以，她用一种聪明的方式让他们在不知不觉中把公园清理干净了。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ for speaking because it uses natural connectors (“actually,” “the funny part,” “so”), varied sentence length, and an engaging, storytelling tone. It also demonstrates fluency and lexical variety (“competitive,” “motivated,” “clever way”).

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-20

**What did Lin tell the runners?**

Lin told the runners that the event was organized as a race in which **participants** were **expected to** collect trash, and the **individual** who gathered the most would be **declared** the winner. This explanation made the activity appear **structured and competitive**, encouraging the runners to participate enthusiastically. **Nevertheless**, it was only **a partial truth** — Lin **deliberately** **omitted** the fact that no actual prize existed. By **presenting the clean-up as a contest**, she effectively motivated the group to contribute, which ultimately resulted in the park being **thoroughly cleaned** in a very short time.

Lin 告诉跑步的人，这个活动被组织成一场比赛，参与者需要捡垃圾，而捡得最多的人将被宣布为赢家。这种解释让活动显得更有条理和竞争性，从而激励了跑步的人积极参与。然而，这只是部分事实 — Lin 刻意省略了根本没有奖品的事实。通过把清理包装成一场竞赛，她有效地激励了大家的参与，最终使公园在很短的时间内被彻底清理干净。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ for writing because it has clear structure, formal academic tone, and complex sentences with subordinating clauses. It avoids repetition, uses precise vocabulary (“participants,” “declared,” “deliberately omitted,” “ultimately”), and demonstrates strong coherence and cohesion.

### Practice History

#### 2025-08-20

**What did Lin tell the runners?**

Helpful Phrases: Lin told/ part of/ could win

Lin told the runners that there was a race **to pick up** trash. If someone picked **up** the most of the trash, they **could** win the race. However, that was **only** part of the truth; Lin didn't mention that there was no prize for the race.

> Corrections:

1. Replaced “If someone picked up the most of the trash” → more natural: “Whoever collected the most trash”.
2. Changed “they could win the race” → smoother integration: “could win the race”.
3. Changed “that was only part of the truth;” → better punctuation with em dash (your preference): “that was only part of the truth —”.

Lin told the runners that there was a race to pick up trash. **Whoever** **collected** the most trash could win the race. However, that was only part of the truth — Lin didn’t mention that there was no prize.

Lin 告诉跑步的人这是一场捡垃圾的比赛。谁捡得最多，就能赢得比赛。然而，这只是部分事实 — Lin 没有提到比赛并没有奖品。
