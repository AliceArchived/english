# Gym Troubles - B3

Bea and Eddy are at the gym, working out together.

- **Eddy**: Time for **squats**! Watch how it's done, Bea.

Eddy grabs a **barbell** and starts squatting.

- **Bea**: Careful, Eddy! Don't hurt yourself.

Eddy squats lower and lower. Suddenly, he **freezes mid-squat**.

- **Eddy**: Uh… Bea. I think I **pulled something**.

Eddy slowly puts the barbell down and stands up, **holding** his back.

- **Bea**: I told you to watch out for injuries! You always **push too hard**.
- **Eddy**: I wasn't pushing too hard. I was just… um… testing my limits.
- **Bea**: Yes, well, your limits **tested back**, and they won.

Bea **smirks** and **pats** Eddy on the shoulder.

- **Bea**: Alright, let's stretch. You've got to **loosen** your back.

Bea gets on the floor to demonstrate a stretch. She **bends forward**.

- **Bea**: See? This stretch **helps with tight muscles**. Try it!

Eddy sits down and tries to copy her. Suddenly, Bea stops moving.

- **Bea**: Oh no. My back is locked!

Bea tries to get up, but can't move. Eddy stares, **wide-eyed**.

- **Eddy**: Wait, are you injured now?
- **Bea**: I think so. What is happening to us?

Eddy slowly stands, **wincing**, and helps Bea up.

- **Bea**: Maybe we need to stop pushing so hard.
- **Eddy**: Yeah… Or maybe we just stick to **cardio**. You can't get injured walking, right?
- **Bea**: Don't **jinx** it, Eddy. Let's get some **ice packs** — before we injure something else!

## Chinese

健身房的麻烦

Bea 和 Eddy 在健身房一起锻炼。

- **Eddy**：深蹲时间！看看该怎么做，Bea。

Eddy 抓起一个**杠铃**，开始深蹲。

- **Bea**：小心点，Eddy！别伤到自己。

Eddy 蹲得越来越低。突然，他停在半蹲的位置。

- **Eddy**：呃……Bea。我好像拉伤了什么。

Eddy 慢慢把杠铃放下，站起来捂着背。

- **Bea**：我就说要小心受伤！你总是太逞强。
- **Eddy**：我没有太逞强。我只是……呃……在测试我的极限。
- **Bea**：是啊，不过你的极限反击了，而且赢了。

Bea 坏笑着拍了拍 Eddy 的肩膀。

- **Bea**：好了，咱们拉伸一下。你得放松你的背部。

Bea 坐到地上示范一个拉伸动作。她向前弯下身。

- **Bea**：看到了吗？这个拉伸有助于缓解肌肉紧张。你也试试！

Eddy 坐下来模仿她。突然，Bea 停住了动作。

- **Bea**：哦，不。我的背僵住了！

Bea 试着站起来，但动不了。Eddy 瞪大眼睛盯着她。

- **Eddy**：等下，你现在受伤了吗？
- **Bea**：我想是的。我们这是怎么了？

Eddy 慢慢站起身，**皱着脸**，扶起 Bea。

- **Bea**：也许我们该别再太拼了。
- **Eddy**：是啊……或者我们干脆只做有氧。走路总不会受伤吧？
- **Bea**：别**乌鸦嘴**啊，Eddy。咱们去拿些冰袋——免得再伤到别的地方！

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**Why does Eddy stop squatting?**

Eddy stops squatting because he suddenly feels a sharp pain and believes he has pulled a muscle in his back. It looks like he was trying to push himself too far, and his body just couldn’t **take** it anymore. To be honest, I think this happens to a lot of people at the gym — they want to test their limits, but sometimes **the limits push back**. In Eddy’s case, he had to stop squatting immediately to avoid making the injury worse.

Eddy 停止深蹲，因为他突然感到剧烈的疼痛，并认为自己拉伤了背部的肌肉。看起来他是想把自己逼得太紧，而身体已经承受不了。说实话，这种情况在健身房很常见——很多人想挑战极限，但有时候极限会“反击”。在 Eddy 的情况里，他必须立刻停止深蹲，以免伤势加重。

> Explanation:

This Speaking-style version is conversational, includes fillers like “to be honest”, uses idiomatic language (“the limits push back”), and explains the situation with personal reflection. The length (80+ words), fluency, and natural connectors push it into Band 7+.

Honestly, a lot of people do that at the gym — they make excuses when they get injured because they don’t want to lose face. In this case, Bea clearly sees through his excuse.

我认为，当 Eddy 说自己是在“测试极限”时，其实只是找借口掩盖他把自己逼得太紧的事实。在我看来，他不想承认 Bea 是对的，所以才让这一切听起来好像是他有意为之。说实话，很多人在健身房都会这样——受伤时找借口，因为他们不想丢面子。在这个情境里，Bea 显然一眼就看穿了他的借口。

> Explanation:

This Speaking-style answer is conversational, reflective, and includes idiomatic expressions like “cover up,” “lose face,” and “see through.” It also uses natural connectors (“to me,” “honestly”). The storytelling tone and personal comment push it into Band 7+.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

**Why does Eddy stop squatting?**

Eddy stops squatting because he **experiences** **sudden pain** and believes he has pulled a muscle in his back. The story suggests that he was attempting to push his physical limits, but his body could no longer support the movement. This situation illustrates a common problem in fitness training: people often ignore their body’s warning signs in order to achieve faster results. Eddy’s decision to stop squatting is both necessary and wise, as continuing could have led to a more serious injury. Ultimately, the episode highlights the importance of training safely and respecting personal limits.

Eddy 停止深蹲是因为他突然感到疼痛，并认为自己拉伤了背部的肌肉。故事表明他当时正试图挑战身体的极限，但身体已经无法支撑动作。这种情况体现了健身训练中的一个常见问题：人们常常忽视身体的警告信号，以求更快的成果。Eddy 选择停止深蹲既必要又明智，因为继续下去可能会导致更严重的伤害。最终，这一情节强调了安全训练和尊重个人极限的重要性。

> Explanation:

This Writing-style version is formal, analytical, and coherent. It uses academic connectors (“however,” “ultimately”), precise vocabulary (“illustrates,” “necessary and wise,” “emphasizes”), and develops ideas logically. With over 100 words, clear grammar, and a well-structured argument, it achieves Band 7+.

Overall, the exchange illustrates the subtle ways people use language to protect their pride even in casual situations.

当 Eddy 声称自己只是“在测试极限”时，这更像是借口而不是一个真正的解释。从上下文来看，Bea 已经提醒过他不要过度用力，而他的回答似乎是一种防御性的方式来保全面子。这反映了人类常见的倾向——与其承认错误，不如找理由合理化。Bea 犀利的回应——“你的极限反击了，而且赢了”——凸显了她看穿了他的借口。整体来看，这段对话展示了人们即使在日常场景中，也会通过语言的微妙方式来维护自尊。

### Practice History

**Why does Eddy stop squatting?**

Helpful Phrases: Eddy stops/ pulls something/ his back

Eddy stops squatting because he thinks he **pulled** something and his back was reached its limit.

> Corrections:

1. Changed “was reached” → “has reached” (correct tense and subject-verb agreement).
2. Added “that” before “his back” for smoother sentence structure.
3. Used present perfect (has pulled, has reached) since the injury has an effect on the present.

Eddy stops squatting because he thinks he **has pulled** something and **that** his back **has reached** its limit.

Eddy 停止深蹲，因为他认为自己拉伤了什么，而且他的背部已经到了极限。
