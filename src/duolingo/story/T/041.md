# The Extra Science Class - B3

Lily's School Newspaper Column

Our school just added an extra science class. It's for students who really love learning. This class has **advanced science projects and experiments**. Everyone says it's great preparation for college. And the teacher has a master's degree in science, so she really knows a lot. She is known for her **dedication to teaching** and **academic excellence**.

I hate it. Some people think more science is better. But not me. Science is like a horror movie where I am always the **victim**. I do not want extra science in my life.

Everyone is talking about how this class will help us get into good colleges. But I do not plan to go to college just so I can attend more science classes. I might have to listen to my parents talk about it, though. They will think it's a great opportunity. I cannot wait.

My friend Zari is excited and planning to join the class. She claims it will be fun and help her with future goals. So she might actually do it. That means she will have less time to hang out with me and Harold. And more time spent talking about science. Fun.

I suggest the school cancels the extra science class. Yes, some students might **miss out on** learning and great opportunities. But I really don't need Zari trying to **convince** me to do more science. Bad news.

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-21

**How do you feel about Lily's opinion on science?**

**Personally**, I disagree with Lily’s view **on** science. I understand that she finds the subject difficult and maybe even stressful, but I believe the extra class is valuable. Science is important nowadays because it develops **problem-solving skills** and **gives** students **an advantage** when applying to college. In fact, many careers **are connected to** science and technology, so having more opportunities to learn is a **benefit**. If Lily doesn’t enjoy it, she doesn’t have to take it, but other students should still have the chance.

就我个人而言，我不同意 Lily 对科学的看法。我理解她觉得这门学科困难甚至有压力，但我认为额外的课程是有价值的。科学在当今非常重要，因为它培养解决问题的能力，并在申请大学时给学生带来优势。事实上，许多职业都与科学和技术相关，因此拥有更多学习的机会是一种好处。如果 Lily 不喜欢，她可以不选，但其他学生仍然应该有这个机会。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ for Speaking because it:

- Uses clear personal opinion with justification (“Personally, I disagree…”).
- Connects ideas smoothly with linking words (because, so, in fact).
- Contains variety in sentence length and vocabulary (advantage, valuable, opportunity).
- Shows ability to discuss both sides before giving a final opinion.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-21

**How do you feel about Lily's opinion on science?**

I **do not agree with** Lily’s opinion about the science class. Although I **respect** her feelings and **recognize** that some students may find science stressful or uninteresting, I believe the school made the right decision to introduce this program. **In today’s world**, science education is **essential**, not only for **gaining admission to** competitive colleges but also for **preparing** students for careers in technology and innovation. Offering more opportunities **benefits the majority of learners** and encourages them to think critically. If Lily does not enjoy it, she can simply **opt out**, but the class should remain available for others who value it.

我不同意 Lily 对科学课的看法。虽然我尊重她的感受，并且承认有些学生可能觉得科学有压力或无趣，但我认为学校推出这个课程的决定是正确的。在当今世界，科学教育是必不可少的，它不仅有助于学生进入竞争激烈的大学，也能为他们未来在科技和创新领域的职业做准备。提供更多机会能让大多数学习者受益，并鼓励他们进行批判性思考。如果 Lily 不喜欢，她可以选择不参加，但课程应继续为其他重视它的学生保留。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ for Writing because it:

- Shows balance (acknowledges Lily’s perspective, then gives counter-argument).
- Uses formal and academic tone (essential, competitive colleges, prepare for careers, critical thinking).
- Contains clear structure with introduction, reasoning, and conclusion.
- Demonstrates variety in sentence length and advanced connectors (although, not only… but also, while, however).

---

- **“opt out”** 的确更常见于**书面语**和**正式表达**（尤其是说明、规章制度、学术写作里），意思是“选择退出，不参加”。在日常口语里也能听到，但用得没那么频繁，人们更常说 **“not take it”**, **“decide not to join”**, **“choose not to do it”**。

  在 IELTS 里：

  - **Speaking** → 用 “choose not to take it” 或 “decide not to join” 更自然，贴近口语。
  - **Writing** → 用 “opt out” 很合适，简洁正式，符合学术写作的风格。

  所以我在 Speaking 版本用了 **“she doesn’t have to take it”**，而在 Writing 版本用了 **“she can simply opt out”**，就是为了体现**口语与书面语的风格差异**。
