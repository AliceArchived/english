# Trapped in the Classroom - B3

Oscar and Eddy are in the comic book-themed classroom for Career Day. The walls are covered with posters of superheroes.

Eddy locks the door by accident while trying to '**strike a superhero pose**.'

- **Oscar**: Eddy! Why did you lock the door?!
- **Eddy**: Oh no! My bad, Oscar. **I thought I was saving the day!**
- **Oscar**: Saving the day? You LOCKED us in!

Oscar tries the door handle. It doesn't **budge**.

- **Oscar**: Great. Just great. Now I'm stuck in here with you.
- **Eddy**: Look on the bright side. It's like we're superheroes trapped in **a villain's lair**!

Eddy grabs a poster of a superhero.

- **Eddy**: If you could have any superpower, Oscar, what would it be?
- **Oscar**: My superpower would be turning invisible… so I wouldn't have to look at you!
- **Eddy**: Ha! Good one! I'd want to fly — **zoom around** saving people!
- **Oscar**: Well, I'd fly out of this classroom, far away from YOU.

Oscar looks at the window.

- **Oscar**: I'm getting out of here. I'll climb through the window.
- **Eddy**: Wait! That's dangerous!

Oscar ignores Eddy and climbs onto the desk under the window.

He **pushes the window open** and climbs out — straight into a trash bin outside.

Students in the yard see him and laugh. Eddy **leans out the window**.

- **Eddy**: Hold on, citizen! I'll save you.

Eddy runs outside and helps Oscar out of the trash bin.

- **Oscar**: I don't need saving, Eddy!
- **Eddy**: Hmm, it didn't look like that from here, Oscar.
- **Oscar**: Next time, I'm locking YOU in the classroom.

## Chinese

被困在教室里

Oscar 和 Eddy 正在职业日的漫画主题教室里。墙上贴满了超级英雄的海报。

Eddy 在尝试“摆超级英雄姿势”时，不小心锁上了门。

- **Oscar**：Eddy！你为什么把门锁上了？！
- **Eddy**：哦不！是我的错，Oscar。我以为我在拯救世界！
- **Oscar**：拯救世界？你是把我们锁在里面了！

Oscar 试了试门把手。它一点也**动不了**。

- **Oscar**：太好了，真是太好了。现在我要和你困在一起。
- **Eddy**：从好的一面看，这就像我们是被困在反派巢穴里的超级英雄！

Eddy 抓起一张超级英雄的海报。

- **Eddy**：如果你能拥有任何超能力，Oscar，你会选择什么？
- **Oscar**：我的超能力会是隐身……这样我就不用看见你了！
- **Eddy**：哈！说得好！我会选择飞行 —— 嗖地飞来飞去拯救人们！
- **Oscar**：好吧，那我就飞出这个教室，离你远远的。

Oscar 看向窗户。

- **Oscar**：我要离开这里。我会爬出去。
- **Eddy**：等等！那很危险！

Oscar 无视 Eddy，爬上窗下的桌子。

他推开窗户爬了出去 —— 直接掉进了外面的垃圾桶。

院子里的学生们看见了，笑了起来。Eddy 探出窗外。

- **Eddy**：坚持住，市民！我来救你。

Eddy 跑到外面，把 Oscar 从垃圾桶里拉了出来。

- **Oscar**：我不需要你来救，Eddy！
- **Eddy**：嗯，从这里看可不是这样，Oscar。
- **Oscar**：下次，我要把你锁在教室里。

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-20

**Why did Oscar climb out the window?**

Well, Oscar climbed through the classroom window because he was **so desperate to** escape from Eddy. He clearly found Eddy annoying and just wanted to get away as quickly as possible. Unfortunately, he didn’t notice there was a trash bin right outside the window, so instead of making a **heroic** escape, he **landed** straight in it. The whole scene was quite embarrassing for him, especially since other students saw him and laughed, while Eddy teased him by pretending to “save” him.

嗯，Oscar 爬出教室的窗户，是因为他实在太想摆脱埃迪了。他觉得埃迪很烦，只想尽快离开。不幸的是，他没有注意到窗外正好有一个垃圾桶，所以他并没有完成一次英勇的逃脱，反而直接掉了进去。整个场面对他来说非常尴尬，尤其是其他同学看见后还在笑，而埃迪则假装要“救”他来取笑他。

> Explanation:

This version is **Band 7+ for Speaking** because it:

- Uses **natural connectors** (“Well… because… unfortunately… instead of… especially since”).
- Adds **detail and emotion** (desperate, annoying, embarrassing).
- Keeps a **spoken, conversational tone** (e.g., “the whole scene was quite embarrassing”).

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-20

**Why did Oscar climb out the window?**

Oscar decided to **climb through** the classroom window because he was eager to escape from Eddy, whose behavior he found **irritating**. His attempt to leave, however, did not **go** as planned. Without noticing the trash bin directly outside, he climbed out and accidentally landed in it. This incident not only **ruined** his escape but also caused him **considerable embarrassment**, as the other students **witnessed** the scene and laughed at him. To make matters worse, Eddy rushed outside and pretended to rescue him, which further **highlighted** Oscar’s **awkward situation**.

Oscar 之所以决定爬出教室的窗户，是因为他急于摆脱让他觉得烦人的埃迪。然而，他的逃脱计划并没有按照预期进行。由于没有注意到窗外正好有一个垃圾桶，他爬出去后不小心掉了进去。这一幕不仅让他的逃跑彻底失败，还让他十分尴尬，因为其他同学目睹了整个过程并取笑他。更糟糕的是，埃迪还跑出去假装要救他，这让 Oscar 的处境更加尴尬。

> Explanation:

This version is **Band 7+ for Writing** because it:

- Uses **formal tone** and precise vocabulary (“eager to escape,” “considerable embarrassment,” “highlighted his awkward situation”).
- Employs **complex sentences** with subordinating clauses and connectors (“however,” “without noticing,” “which further…”).
- Expands the idea logically, demonstrating **cohesion and coherence**.

### Speaking vs Writing Feature Comparison

#### 2025-08-20

| Feature                     | Speaking Version                                           | Writing Version                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sentence length**         | Short–medium (spoken-friendly)                             | Medium–long, more complex                                                     |
| **Tone**                    | Conversational, casual                                     | Formal, academic                                                              |
| **Common connectors**       | Well, because, unfortunately, instead of, especially since | However, without noticing, not only…but also, to make matters worse           |

### Practice History

#### 2025-08-20

**Why did Oscar climb out the window?**

Helpful Phrases: Oscar climbed/ escape / from Eddy

Oscar climbed through the classroom window to escape from Eddy. But he didn't notice the trash bin and jumped into it.

> Corrections:

1. Combined into one smooth sentence with a comma before but (better flow).
2. Changed “jumped into it” → “ended up falling into it” (closer to the original story: Oscar climbed out and unintentionally landed in the trash bin, not deliberately jumped).

Oscar climbed through the classroom window to escape from Eddy, but he didn’t notice the trash bin and **ended up** **falling into** it.

Oscar 爬出了教室的窗户想要摆脱埃迪，但他没有注意到垃圾桶，结果掉了进去。
