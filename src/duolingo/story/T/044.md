# The Glass Wave - B3

Lucy and Oscar enter **a grand art gallery**. The room is full of sculptures.

- **Oscar**: Why are we here, Lucy? These exhibits never show good art.
- **Lucy**: Oh, stop. This exhibit **features** the most impressive ones from **rising sculptors**.
- **Oscar**: I'm already unimpressed. I doubt they'll ever **match** my talent.
- **Lucy**: Perhaps you can learn something. You always say good art should **inspire**.

Oscar **rolls his eyes** but follows Lucy deeper into the gallery.

He sees a sculpture that looks like a giant glass wave.

- **Oscar**: Hmm. This one isn't as bad as the others. It almost captured something real.

Oscar picks up a glass of red wine from a nearby table.

- **Lucy**: Be careful with your wine, Oscar. These sculptures aren't cheap.
- **Oscar**: Lucy, I'm always careful.

Suddenly, Oscar **gestures grandly** — and spills the wine on the glass wave.

- **Oscar**: Oh no!

Lucy gasps.

- **Lucy**: Oscar, what did you do?!
- **Oscar**: Wait… I've got an idea.

Oscar steps in front of the sculpture, **spreading his arms**. People start noticing.

- **Oscar**: This is my newest work! I call it "The Red Wave." It's about **chaos and beauty**.

The gallery **curator** watches, nodding thoughtfully. He approaches Oscar.

Later, outside the gallery, Lucy **smirks** at Oscar.

- **Lucy**: Well, you **got lucky**. That curator loved your "art" and wants to **feature you**.
- **Oscar**: Of course! True talent always stands out.
- **Lucy**: True talent… or accidental spills.
- **Oscar**: Either way, Lucy, I'm unforgettable!

## Chinese

玻璃之浪

Lucy 和 Oscar 走进一间宏伟的艺术画廊。房间里满是雕塑。

- **Oscar**：我们为什么在这里，Lucy？这些展品从来没有真正的好艺术。
- **Lucy**：哦，别这样。这次展览展示的是**新晋雕塑家**中最令人印象深刻的作品。
- **Oscar**：我已经毫无兴趣了。我怀疑他们永远也比不上我的才华。
- **Lucy**：也许你能学到点东西。你总说好的艺术应该能带来灵感。

Oscar 翻了个白眼，但还是跟着 Lucy 深入画廊。

他看到一件看起来像巨大的玻璃浪潮的雕塑。

- **Oscar**：嗯，这个不像其他的那么糟。它几乎捕捉到了某种真实的东西。

Oscar 从附近的桌子上拿起一杯红酒。

- **Lucy**：小心点你的酒，Oscar。这些雕塑可不便宜。
- **Oscar**：Lucy，我一向很小心。

突然，Oscar 夸张地挥手——把红酒洒在了那件玻璃浪潮雕塑上。

- **Oscar**：哦，不！

Lucy 倒吸一口气。

- **Lucy**：Oscar，你做了什么？！
- **Oscar**：等等……我有个主意。

Oscar 站到雕塑前，张开双臂。人们开始注意到他。

- **Oscar**：这是我的最新作品！我称之为《红色浪潮》。它讲的是混乱与美。

画廊的**策展人**注视着，若有所思地点点头。他走向 Oscar。

稍后，在画廊外，露西**坏笑着**看向 Oscar。

- **Lucy**：好吧，你运气不错。那个策展人很喜欢你的“艺术”，还想推你出名。
- **Oscar**：当然！真正的天赋总会脱颖而出。
- **Lucy**：真正的天赋……还是意外的泼洒？
- **Oscar**：无论哪种，Lucy，我都是令人难忘的！

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**What did Oscar call his "newest work"?**

Oscar said his newest work was called “The Red Wave.” The name came about because he accidentally spilled red wine on a glass sculpture in the gallery. Instead of admitting the mistake, he quickly pretended it was intentional and explained it as a creative expression of chaos and beauty. I think it was quite funny, because his act turned an accident into something that people admired. Honestly, it shows how confidence can sometimes convince others of your talent.

Oscar 说他最新的作品叫《红色波浪》。这个名字的由来是因为他不小心把红酒洒在了画廊里的玻璃雕塑上。他没有承认错误，而是迅速假装这是有意的，并把它解释为对混乱与美的创造性表达。我觉得这很有趣，因为他的行为把一次意外变成了让人欣赏的“艺术”。老实说，这说明自信有时候真的能让别人相信你的才华。

> Explanation:

This version reaches Band 7+ because:

1. It uses extended sentences with linking words (“instead of,” “because,” “honestly”).
2. It includes personal opinion and evaluation (“I think it was quite funny,” “it shows how confidence…”).
3. Vocabulary is varied and natural for speaking (e.g., “came about,” “pretended,” “turned an accident into something admired”).

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

**What did Oscar call his "newest work"?**

Oscar called his newest work “The Red Wave,” a title that originated from an accident in the gallery. While examining a glass sculpture, he spilled red wine on it by mistake. Instead of admitting fault, he quickly turned the incident into an opportunity by presenting it as his own **artistic creation**. He explained the piece as **a reflection of chaos and beauty**, and surprisingly, the curator accepted this explanation. This episode illustrates not only the **subjectivity** of art but also how confidence and **improvisation** can transform an error into **recognition**.

Oscar 把他最新的作品称为《红色波浪》，这个名字源自于在画廊发生的一次意外。当他欣赏一件玻璃雕塑时，不小心把红酒洒在了上面。他没有承认过失，而是迅速把这件事转化为机会，声称这是他自己的艺术创作。他把作品解释为对混乱与美的反映，而令人惊讶的是，策展人接受了这个说法。这一情节不仅说明了艺术的主观性，也展示了自信与临场应变如何能把错误转化为认可。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ because:

- It has **formal tone and structure**, appropriate for writing.
- Uses **complex sentences** with clear connectors (“instead of,” “while,” “not only…but also”).
- Vocabulary is more academic (e.g., “originated,” “illustrates,” “recognition,” “subjectivity”).
- Provides a **logical conclusion** about the meaning of the event, beyond retelling.

### Practice History

**What did Oscar call his "newest work"?**

Helpful Phrases: Oscar called/ newest work/ Red Wave

Oscar called his newest work the Red Wave because he **accidentally spilled** red wine onto the sculpture and he acted this way to cover his mistake.

> Corrections:

1. dded quotation marks around “The Red Wave” to match the original story.
2. Inserted a comma before “and” to correctly join two independent clauses.

Oscar called his newest work **“The Red Wave”** because he accidentally spilled red wine onto the sculpture, and he acted this way to cover his mistake.

Oscar 称他最新的作品为《红色波浪》，因为他不小心把红酒洒在雕塑上，并且这样表现是为了掩饰自己的错误。
