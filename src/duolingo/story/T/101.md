# The Vacuum Debate - B3

Eddy and Junior are in the living room. Eddy points to the vacuum cleaner.

- **Eddy**: Junior, time to vacuum the floor. It's a mess in here.
- **Junior**: I can't. The noise will ruin my video game **vibes**.
- **Eddy**: Your 'vibes'? What does that even mean?
- **Junior**: Dad, in my game, the hero needs focus. If I vacuum, we'll lose the battle!

Eddy looks at Junior, confused but amused.

- **Eddy**: So, your game hero depends on a **quiet** house?
- **Junior**: Exactly. No noise, no stress! **Quiet** means victory!

Eddy scratches his head.

- **Eddy**: Fine, no vacuuming… for now.

Eddy heads to the kitchen. Junior keeps playing his game.

A little while later, Eddy comes back with a big bag.

- **Junior**: What's in the bag?
- **Eddy**: Popcorn! It's movie night.
- **Junior**: Yes! Can I have some?

Eddy shakes his head.

- **Eddy**: Nope. I can't share. Crumbs will ruin the movie night vibes.
- **Junior**: What?! That's not the same thing!
- **Eddy**: Oh, I think it is. **Quiet** vibes. Movie vibes. Same rule.

Junior puts down his controller.

- **Junior**: Fine, I'll vacuum. But this 'vibes' thing? Terrible rule, Dad.
- **Eddy**: **Says the kid who invented it!**

## Vocabulary

- vibe: 英 /vaib/, 美 /vaɪb/
  - n. ①(感情上的)感应，共鸣 ②(一地的)气氛，环境
  - vi. (感情上)产生共鸣

## Chinese

吸尘器的争论

Eddy 和 Junior 在客厅里。Eddy 指着吸尘器。

- **Eddy**: Junior，该吸地板了。这里乱成一团。
- **Junior**: 我不行。噪音会破坏我电子游戏的**氛围**。
- **Eddy**: 你的“氛围”？那到底是什么意思？
- **Junior**: 爸，在我的游戏里，英雄需要专注。如果我吸尘，我们就会输掉战斗！

Eddy 看着 Junior，困惑但觉得好笑。

- **Eddy**: 所以，你的游戏英雄需要一个**安静**的房子？
- **Junior**: 没错。没有噪音，就没有压力！**安静**意味着胜利！

Eddy 挠了挠头。

- **Eddy**: 好吧，不吸地…暂时不吸。

Eddy 走向厨房。Junior 继续玩游戏。

过了一会儿，Eddy 拎着一个大袋子回来。

- **Junior**: 袋子里是什么？
- **Eddy**: 爆米花！是电影之夜。
- **Junior**: 太好了！我能吃点吗？

Eddy 摇了摇头。

- **Eddy**: 不行。我不能分享。碎屑会破坏电影之夜的氛围。
- **Junior**: 什么？！那不一样！
- **Eddy**: 哦，我觉得一样。**安静**的氛围。电影氛围。同一个规则。

Junior 放下了手柄。

- **Junior**: 好吧，我来吸尘。但这个“氛围”的规则？糟透了，爸。
- **Eddy**: **这话可是你自己发明的！**

## Sentences

- 我不行。那些噪音会破坏我打游戏的氛围感。
  - I can't. The noise will ruin my video game **vibes**.
- 这不是你自己发明的规矩吗！
  - Says the kid who invented it!
  - 这句话是一个英语中的常见句型结构，省略了主句，其完整形式其实是：
    - That’s what you're saying, but you’re the kid who invented it!
    - 你现在在说这个，但这规矩明明是你发明的！
  - Says the kid = 说这话的人是那个孩子
  - who invented it = （他）自己发明了这个东西（规则/借口/概念）
  - Says the person who was late yesterday!
    - 你昨天才迟到好不好，现在还说别人？

## Speaking

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-24

**Why does Junior say he can't vacuum?**

Junior says he can’t vacuum because he believes **the sound of the machine** will completely ruin the atmosphere he needs for his video game. He even explains that his game hero requires total concentration and silence in order to win battles. From his point of view, any extra noise is **a major distraction**. That’s why he insists that vacuuming is impossible at that moment, since it would immediately break the “game vibes” he’s trying to protect.

Junior 说他不能吸尘，因为他认为吸尘器的声音会彻底破坏他玩游戏所需要的氛围。他还解释说，他的游戏英雄需要完全的专注和安静才能赢得战斗。在他看来，任何额外的噪音都是巨大的干扰。这就是为什么他坚持此刻不能吸尘，因为这会立刻破坏他努力维持的“游戏氛围”。

> Explanation:

This version reaches Band 7+ for Speaking because it uses **extended sentences**, **clear reasoning**, and **expressive vocabulary** (e.g., “atmosphere,” “major distraction,” “immediately break”). It maintains a natural spoken tone with linking phrases like _“From his point of view”_ and _“That’s why.”_

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-24

**Why does Junior say he can't vacuum?**

Junior refuses to vacuum because he **argues** that the noise would **interfere with** the atmosphere he relies on to play his video game. He explains that his hero within the game requires silence and focus in order to achieve victory. According to his reasoning, any external sound, such as the vacuum, would immediately disrupt this focus and ultimately cause failure in the game. Therefore, Junior insists that vacuuming is not possible while he is playing, as the action would destroy the carefully maintained “game vibes” that he believes are **essential** for success.

Junior 拒绝吸尘，因为他认为噪音会干扰他玩电子游戏所依赖的氛围。他解释说，游戏中的英雄需要安静和专注才能取得胜利。根据他的逻辑，任何外部声音，例如吸尘器的噪音，都会立即打断这种专注，并最终导致游戏失败。因此，Junior 坚持在玩游戏时不能吸尘，因为这个动作会破坏他认为对成功至关重要的“游戏氛围”。

> Explanation:

This Writing version is Band 7+ because it demonstrates **formal academic tone**, **precise vocabulary** (“interfere,” “disrupt,” “ultimately cause failure”), and **logical progression** with connectors like _“According to his reasoning”_ and _“Therefore.”_ The structure is coherent, with a clear cause–effect chain, showing task fulfillment at a high level.
