# The Messy Mailroom - B3

Oscar and Eddy are in the school's tiny, **overstuffed** mailroom. Packages and letters are everywhere.

- **Eddy**: Wow, so many packages! This is the messiest mailroom I've ever seen.
- **Oscar**: Yes, thank you for that **observation**, Eddy. I'm glad you can see what's right in front of us.
- **Eddy**: Do you think they ever organize these? Like, by student or teacher? Or are the mailboxes just… **random**?
- **Oscar**: Eddy, this isn't the time for your endless **commentary** about mailboxes. Let's find a way out of here!

Oscar tries the door, but it doesn't open.

- **Eddy**: Huh, we're stuck. No problem! Someone will find us — eventually.
- **Oscar**: Eventually? I can't stay in here forever!
- **Eddy**: Relax, Oscar. When in doubt, **stop stressing out**!
- **Oscar**: Stop stressing out? I am trapped with a man who is constantly talking!
- **Eddy**: Hey, I'm just **keeping things positive**. You'd miss me if I weren't here.
- **Oscar**: I'd miss silence more.

Oscar starts climbing over a pile of packages.

- **Eddy**: What are you doing? That's a bad idea, Oscar.
- **Oscar**: It's better than listening to you talk!

Oscar **slips** and **falls** to the ground.

- **Oscar**: Ow! My wrist!
- **Eddy**: I told you! Now you're hurt.
- **Oscar**: Great, Eddy. What am I supposed to do now?
- **Eddy**: Don't worry, buddy. I'll help you. I'll even help you teach your art class this week while your wrist recovers!
- **Oscar**: My poor students…

## Chinese

凌乱的邮件室

Oscar 和 Eddy 在学校狭小而塞满东西的邮件室里。包裹和信件到处都是。

- **Eddy**：哇，这么多包裹！这是我见过最乱的邮件室了。
- **Oscar**：是的，谢谢你的观察，Eddy。我很高兴你能看见我们眼前的东西。
- **Eddy**：你觉得他们会整理这些吗？比如按学生或者老师？还是这些信箱只是……随便的？
- **Oscar**：Eddy，现在不是你对信箱无休止**评论**的时候。我们得想办法出去！

Oscar 试了试门，但门打不开。

- **Eddy**：啊，我们被困住了。没问题！有人会找到我们的 —— 最终。
- **Oscar**：最终？我不能永远待在这里！
- **Eddy**：放轻松，Oscar。当你不确定时，就别紧张！
- **Oscar**：别紧张？我可是被一个不停说话的人困住了！
- **Eddy**：嘿，我只是在保持积极的气氛。如果没有我，你会想念我的。
- **Oscar**：我会更想念安静。

Oscar 开始爬上一堆包裹。

- **Eddy**：你在干什么？这是个糟糕的主意，Oscar。
- **Oscar**：总比听你说话好！

Oscar 滑倒，摔在地上。

- **Oscar**：哎哟！我的手腕！
- **Eddy**：我早就告诉你了！现在你受伤了。
- **Oscar**：太好了，Eddy。我现在该怎么办？
- **Eddy**：别担心，伙计。我会帮你的。我甚至会帮你代课，你这周的美术课就由我来上，直到你的手腕恢复！
- **Oscar**：我可怜的学生们……

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-21

**Why did Oscar climb over the packages?**

Well, Oscar decided to **climb over** the pile of packages because he was **desperate** to escape. The door was **stuck**, so he felt **trapped** inside the messy mailroom, and climbing seemed like his only option. Even though Eddy kept talking, Oscar just wanted to try something rather than wait forever. I think his action **shows** that sometimes, when people are stressed, they would rather take a risk than stay in an uncomfortable situation.

嗯，Oscar 决定爬过那堆包裹是因为他非常想逃出去。门被卡住了，所以他觉得自己被困在凌乱的邮件室里，而爬过去似乎是唯一的办法。虽然 Eddy 一直在说话，但 Oscar 只是想采取一些行动，而不是无休止地等待。我觉得他的举动说明，有时候人在压力之下宁愿冒险，也不愿意困在不舒服的处境中。

> Explanation:

This version uses spoken connectors (“Well,” “so,” “even though,” “I think”), natural intonation, and explanatory detail. It’s over 80 words, shows reasoning and opinion, and uses idiomatic expressions like “stuck,” “take a risk,” and “uncomfortable situation.” These features fit IELTS Speaking Band 7+: fluency, range of vocabulary, and coherence.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-21

**Why did Oscar climb over the packages?**

Oscar climbed over the packages because he was trying to find a way out of the mailroom. The door would not open, and he felt **trapped** in a very **confined** and **messy** space. Although it was not the safest idea, climbing appeared to be his only choice at that moment. His action reflects a common human response when faced with stress: rather than waiting **passively**, people often attempt something, even if it involves risk. In this case, Oscar’s **frustration** and the **constant chatter** from Eddy may have pushed him to act more quickly than he normally would.

Oscar 爬过那些包裹是因为他想找到离开邮件室的办法。门打不开，他觉得自己被困在一个狭小而凌乱的空间里。虽然这并不是最安全的选择，但在那一刻，攀爬似乎是他唯一的办法。他的举动反映了人在压力下的一种常见反应：与其被动等待，人们往往会尝试采取行动，即使这意味着冒险。在这种情况下，Oscar 的沮丧情绪以及 Eddy 不停的唠叨，可能让他比平时更快地做出了决定。

> Explanation:

This version is formal, over 100 words, with clear sentence structures and logical connectors (“because,” “although,” “rather than,” “in this case”). It shows analysis (human response under stress), uses academic vocabulary (“confined,” “reflects,” “frustration”), and maintains coherence. These are Writing Band 7+ features.

### Speaking vs Writing Feature Comparison

#### 2025-08-21

| Feature                 | Speaking Version                                                       | Writing Version                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sentence length         | Short to medium (conversational, natural pauses)                       | Longer, more complex with subordinate clauses                               |
| Tone                    | Informal, personal, interactive (“Well,” “I think”)                    | Formal, analytical, objective                                               |
| Common connectors       | Well, so, even though, I think                                         | Because, although, rather than, in this case                                |
| Vocabulary choice       | Everyday words (“stuck,” “take a risk,” “uncomfortable”)               | Academic words (“confined,” “reflects,” “frustration,” “passively”)         |
| Emotional expression    | Shows feelings (“desperate,” “just wanted,” “uncomfortable situation”) | More neutral and evaluative, emotions explained in broader context          |
| Rhetorical/detail style | Personal story-like explanation with opinions                          | Structured argument, explanation of human behavior and logical consequences |

### Practice History

#### 2025-08-21

**Why did Oscar climb over the packages?**

Helpful Phrases: Oscar climbed/ find a /being trapped

Oscar climbed over the packages to find a way to get out of the room. He was trapped in a room full of mailboxes and the door couldn't open.

> Corrections:

1. “to get out of the room” → “to find a way out of the room” (more natural).
2. “a room full of mailboxes” → “the mailroom” (the story context).
3. “the door couldn't open” → “the door would not open” (clearer and more natural in standard English).

Oscar climbed over the packages to find **a way out of the room**. He was trapped in the **mailroom**, and the door **would not open**.

Oscar 爬过那些包裹是为了找到离开房间的办法。他被困在邮件室里，而门打不开。
