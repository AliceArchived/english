# The Neon Sign - B3

Lin walks into Vik's Café with a big box.

- **Vikram**: Good morning, Lin. What's in the box?
- **Lin**: Morning, Vikram. It's a neon sign! I bought it online.
- **Vikram**: A neon sign? Why?
- **Lin**: Advertising! A bright sign will make people notice the café. More customers, more money.
- **Vikram**: It looks… fine. Was it expensive?
- **Lin**: No way. I got a "gently used" one. It was cheap.
- **Vikram**: Hmm. Perhaps we should've planned this better.
- **Lin**: Don't worry! It's perfect. Let me **set it up**.

Lin plugs in the neon sign. It lights up brightly for a moment.

- **Vikram**: Oh, it works! That's good.

Suddenly, the sign **flickers**. Then, all the lights in the café **go out**.

- **Vikram**: Oh no! The power's out! What happened?
- **Lin**: Uh… I think the sign **shorted out the power**.

Customers **groan** and leave the café.

- **Vikram**: Lin, the café just shut down during the busiest hour of the day!
- **Lin**: OK, but think about this: We saved money on the sign.
- **Vikram**: And now we'll spend more fixing the power!

An **electrician** fixes the power later. Lin looks at the sign.

- **Lin**: So… should I plug it in again?
- **Vikram**: No! Please just make tea.
- **Lin**: Fine. But I still think it's a great sign.

## Chinese

霓虹灯牌

Lin 抱着一个大箱子走进维克的咖啡馆。

- **Vikram**：早上好，Lin。箱子里是什么？
- **Lin**：早上好，Vikram。这是个霓虹灯牌！我在网上买的。
- **Vikram**：霓虹灯牌？为什么？
- **Lin**：做广告啊！明亮的灯牌会让人注意到咖啡馆。更多顾客，更多收入。
- **Vikram**：看起来……还不错。贵吗？
- **Lin**：当然不。我买的是“轻微使用过”的二手货，很便宜。
- **Vikram**：嗯，也许我们应该计划得更好一些。
- **Lin**：别担心！它很完美。让我来安装一下。

Lin 插上霓虹灯的电源。灯牌亮了一下。

- **Vikram**：哦，它能亮！不错。

突然，灯牌**闪烁**了一下。接着，咖啡馆里的所有灯都灭了。

- **Vikram**：糟了！停电了！发生什么事了？
- **Lin**：呃……我想是灯牌让电路短路了。

顾客们发出抱怨声，离开了咖啡馆。

- **Vikram**：Lin，咖啡馆在一天中最忙的时候停业了！
- **Lin**：好吧，但想想看：我们在灯牌上省了钱。
- **Vikram**：可现在我们要花更多的钱来修电路！

稍后，一位**电工**修好了电路。Lin 看着灯牌。

- **Lin**：那么……我现在能再插上它吗？
- **Vikram**：不！请你去泡茶吧。
- **Lin**：好吧。但我还是觉得这是个很棒的灯牌。

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**Why did the power go out in the café?**

The power in the café went out because the neon sign short-circuited and caused an **outage**. It happened right in the middle of the café’s busiest hour, when there were lots of customers inside. Everyone was **caught off guard** — the lights went off suddenly, and some customers even left. It was a bit of a disaster for business that day, all because of **the faulty sign**.

咖啡馆停电是因为霓虹灯短路导致断电。这事正好发生在咖啡馆最忙的时候，店里坐满了顾客。大家都被吓了一跳——灯突然灭了，还有顾客离开。对那天的生意来说，这算是一次小灾难，而这一切都是那块有问题的霓虹灯引起的。

> Explanation:

This version uses natural spoken connectors (“and,” “when,” “all because of”), some expressive phrases (“caught off guard,” “a bit of a disaster”), and a narrative tone suitable for Speaking. The sentences vary in length, creating a conversational rhythm that reflects Band 7+ fluency and coherence.

- caught off guard
  - 措手不及 / 出乎意料，常用来描述某人因突发情况而没有准备。

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

**Why did the power go out in the café?**

The café experienced a power outage because the neon sign short-circuited, which immediately disrupted the electricity supply. This incident occurred during the café’s busiest hour, when many customers were seated and ordering drinks. The sudden loss of lighting and power caused confusion and inconvenience, **prompting** several customers to leave. As a result, business operations were interrupted, leading to a direct loss in sales and productivity. The root cause was the faulty neon sign, which, although purchased cheaply, ultimately cost the café more in repairs than any savings it initially offered.

咖啡馆发生停电是因为霓虹灯短路，立即中断了电力供应。这一事件发生在咖啡馆最忙的时段，许多顾客正在就座并点单。突然的断电和灯光熄灭引起了混乱和不便，导致一些顾客离开。因此，营业被迫中断，造成了直接的销售和生产力损失。根本原因在于那块有缺陷的霓虹灯，虽然购买时很便宜，但最终在维修上的花费远超了最初节省的成本。

> Explanation:

This version uses precise formal vocabulary (“experienced a power outage,” “prompting several customers to leave,” “operations were interrupted”), a clear cause–effect structure, and a logical progression of ideas. It also includes evaluative commentary on the cost-benefit aspect, which adds depth and meets Band 7+ Writing criteria for task achievement, cohesion, and lexical resource.
