# The Quiet Airport - B3

A Review by Vikram

Recently, I had the pleasure of **visiting** an airport. I was there to **catch** a flight and **experience** the joys of travel. Here's what **made** my visit to the airport memorable:

The airport experience started as soon as I arrived. There were no announcements for the flights. In fact, I was surprised when the speakers stayed silent. But it was nice to have quiet moments! It felt peaceful, like **a calming meditation session** before a flight.

Normally, airports are loud and busy. But not this one! This airport was different. The lack of announcements meant everyone ended up talking to each other. I **got to know** other travelers! We shared stories about our trips. It was like a mini meetup! You could say meeting new people was not **on my schedule**! But I enjoyed it a lot.

Without speaker announcements, the staff had to work extra hard. They walked around and told each passenger when it was time for their flight. I asked one staff member about my flight. She politely pointed me in the right direction. It was **a friendly and personal touch** I appreciated.

I look forward to my next trip through this airport. It was different from any airport I've visited before. I would recommend this experience to anyone who enjoys surprises and making new friends.

Five stars for this unique airport!

## Vocabulary

- experience: 英 /ɪk'spɪəriəns/, 美 /ɪk'spɪriəns/
  - n. 经验，体验
    - 经历；阅历；感受，体会
  - vt. 经历，体验；感受，遭受
- Normally, airports are **loud** and busy.
  - loud: noisy

## Chinese

安静的机场

Vikram 的评论

最近，我很高兴参观了一个机场。我是去赶飞机，同时体验旅行的乐趣。以下是让我对这个机场印象深刻的原因：

一到机场，我的体验就开始了。这里没有任何航班广播。事实上，当扬声器保持安静时，我很惊讶。但有安静的时刻真不错！感觉很平和，就像在飞行前的一次冥想。

通常，机场都是嘈杂而忙碌的。但这个机场不是！它与众不同。没有广播意味着每个人最后都开始互相交谈。我认识了其他旅客！我们分享了各自旅行的故事。这就像一个小型聚会！你可以说，认识新朋友并不在我的计划里！但我非常享受。

没有广播，工作人员就必须更加努力。他们走来走去，告诉每位乘客什么时候该登机。我向一位工作人员询问了我的航班。她礼貌地为我指明方向。这种友好而个性化的服务让我很感激。

我期待下一次经过这个机场的旅行。它和我去过的任何机场都不同。我会推荐这种体验给任何喜欢惊喜和结交新朋友的人。

给这个独特的机场五星好评！

## Sentences

- It was **a friendly and personal touch** I appreciated.
  - 这种友好而个性化的服务让我很感激。

a (nice/friendly/personal) touch = 一种细节上的点缀、特别的做法、贴心的安排。

可以理解为：一个小小的举动、细节，使整体体验更温暖、更周到。

所以这里的意思是： “那是一种友好又个性化的 贴心细节，让我很感激。”

### 举例

1. _Fresh flowers on the table were a nice touch._
   桌上的鲜花是一个贴心的点缀。
2. _The staff remembered my name — a personal touch that made me feel welcome._
   员工记住了我的名字 —— 这个细节让我感到宾至如归。
3. _Handwritten notes add a thoughtful touch to gifts._
   手写便条给礼物增添了体贴的感觉。

“touch” 在这里确实可以理解成一种特别的服务、细节。更准确地说，它指的是 让体验变得更好的一点额外心意。

### “Touch” 的常见比喻用法

| 搭配                          | 含义                                | 示例                                                             | 中文理解                         |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **a nice touch**              | 贴心的细节 / 小点缀                 | _The welcome drink was a nice touch._                            | 欢迎饮品是个贴心的细节。         |
| **a personal touch**          | 个性化的处理 / 让人觉得更贴心的细节 | _The handwritten thank-you card added a personal touch._         | 手写的感谢卡增添了个人化的温情。 |
| **a final touch**             | 最后的润色 / 点睛之笔               | _The chef added a sprig of mint as a final touch._               | 厨师加了一枝薄荷作为最后的点缀。 |
| **a human touch**             | 人情味 / 人性化的温度               | _The manager’s speech had a human touch._                        | 经理的讲话充满人情味。           |
| **add the finishing touches** | 做最后润色 / 收尾工作               | _We just need to add the finishing touches to the presentation._ | 我们只需要对演示做最后的润色。   |

#### 总结

在比喻用法里，**touch** ≈ “细节 / 点缀 / 温情处理”，强调 _不是大的动作，而是让体验更完美的“小动作、小心意”_。

## Speaking & Writing

#### 2025-08-26

**What was most unique about Vikram's airport experience?**

I think what **stood out** most about Vikram’s airport experience was the fact that there were **no announcements at all**. **Normally**, airports are noisy and crowded, but this one was **peaceful**, almost like **a meditation session** before the flight. Because of the silence, passengers **naturally** started talking to each other, which made the **atmosphere** more friendly. **At the same time**, the staff worked harder by **personally informing** travelers, which **added a personal touch**. Overall, it was unusual but surprisingly pleasant.

我认为 Vikram 的机场体验中最特别的一点是完全没有任何广播。通常机场都很嘈杂和拥挤，但这里却很安静，几乎像是在登机前进行一次冥想。由于这种安静，乘客们自然开始互相交谈，使氛围更加友好。同时，工作人员则更加努力地逐一通知旅客，这也带来了个人化的体验。总体来说，这虽然不寻常，但却出乎意料地令人愉快。
