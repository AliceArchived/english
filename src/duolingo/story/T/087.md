# The Scavenger Hunt - B3

Lucy is at the table with a basket of papers. Lin walks in, holding a mug of coffee.

- **Lin**: What's all this?
- **Lucy**: I set up **a scavenger hunt** for the neighborhood! You're doing it.
- **Lin**: Me? Why?
- **Lucy**: You need to get outside. You need to connect with people.
- **Lin**: Ugh. What kind of things do I have to find?

Lucy hands Lin a list.

- **Lucy**: A blue lamp, a rock shaped like a heart, and… oh, a person who can juggle.
- **Lin**: A juggler? That sounds unlikely.
- **Lucy**: It's supposed to be fun, Lin. Go!

Lin sighs, but takes the list and heads out.

An hour later, Lin returns to the apartment, empty-handed.

- **Lucy**: Lin, where's your basket of scavenger hunt items?
- **Lin**: I didn't collect anything.
- **Lucy**: What? What were you doing?
- **Lin**: I went to the coffee shop. I showed the barista the scavenger list and asked if I could trade it for free coffee.
- **Lucy**: You what?!
- **Lin**: She gave me a **muffin**, too.

Lucy looks shocked for a moment, then puts her hand on her forehead.

- **Lucy**: I tried so hard to get you outside, and you still found a way to take advantage of this.
- **Lin**: Grandma, you can't fight who I am.
- **Lucy**: I wish I'd given that list to someone else.

## Chinese

寻宝游戏

Lucy 坐在桌边，桌上放着一篮子纸。Lin 端着一杯咖啡走了进来。

- **Lin**：这都是些什么？
- **Lucy**：我为社区组织了一个寻宝游戏！你要参加。
- **Lin**：我？为什么？
- **Lucy**：你需要出去走走，需要和人们交流。
- **Lin**：呃。我得找些什么东西？

Lucy 把一张清单递给 Lin。

- **Lucy**：一盏蓝色的灯、一块心形的石头，还有……哦，一个会玩杂耍的人。
- **Lin**：杂耍演员？这听起来不太可能。
- **Lucy**：这是为了好玩，Lin。去吧！

Lin 叹了口气，但还是拿着清单出门了。

一个小时后，Lin 空手回到了公寓。

- **Lucy**：Lin，你的寻宝篮子呢？
- **Lin**：我什么也没收集到。
- **Lucy**：什么？那你在干什么？
- **Lin**：我去了咖啡店，把寻宝清单给咖啡师看，问能不能用它换杯免费的咖啡。
- **Lucy**：你说什么？！
- **Lin**：她还给了我一个松饼。

Lucy 愣了一下，然后用手扶住额头。

- **Lucy**：我费了那么大劲想让你出去走走，你还是找到了占这个便宜的办法。
- **Lin**：奶奶，你没法改变我的本性。
- **Lucy**：我真希望把那张清单给别人。

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**What did Lin do with the scavenger list?**

Instead of following the scavenger hunt rules, Lin walked into a coffee shop with the list, showed it to the barista, and asked if she could trade it for a cup of coffee. To her surprise, she ended up with both a coffee and a muffin. Honestly, that’s just typical of her — she always finds a way to turn situations to her advantage, no matter what the original plan was. It’s clever, slightly cheeky, and a perfect example of her personality in action.

Lin 没有按寻宝游戏的规则来，而是拿着清单走进咖啡店，给咖啡师看，并问能不能用它换一杯咖啡。出乎意料的是，她最后不仅拿到了咖啡，还拿到了一块松饼。老实说，这就是她的典型作风——无论原计划是什么，她总能找到方法让事情对自己有利。既聪明又有点调皮，这正好展现了她的性格。

> Explanation:

该版本符合 IELTS Speaking Band 7+ 标准：有流畅的叙述、自然的口语连接词（Instead of, To her surprise, Honestly）、多样化的句型结构以及适度的情绪色彩（slightly cheeky, typical of her），展现流利度与连贯性。

- cheeky: 英 /'tʃiːki/, 美 /'tʃiːki/
  - adj. 无耻的；厚脸皮的

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

**What did Lin do with the scavenger list?**

Lin did not approach the scavenger hunt in the **intended manner**. She carried the list into a coffee shop, presented it to the barista, and inquired whether she could exchange it for a cup of coffee. Unexpectedly, the barista provided not only the coffee but also a muffin. This incident illustrates Lin’s ability to **repurpose** a given task for her own benefit, showing both resourcefulness and a **disregard** for **conventional rules**. Rather than pursuing the items on the list, she turned the document into a bargaining tool, transforming an activity meant for exploration into an opportunity for personal gain.

Lin 并没有按照寻宝游戏的本意行事。她把清单带到咖啡店，递给咖啡师，并询问是否可以用它换一杯咖啡。出乎意料的是，咖啡师不仅给了她咖啡，还给了她一个松饼。这件事体现了 Lin 将任务重新利用为自己谋利的能力，既展现了机智，也表现出对常规规则的不在意。她没有去寻找清单上的物品，而是将其变成了谈判工具，把一项旨在探索的活动转化为个人获益的机会。

> Explanation:

该版本符合 IELTS Writing Band 7+：用词正式且精准（inquired, repurpose, bargaining tool）、逻辑结构清晰（先叙述 → 再解释 → 最后总结），同时展示了较高的语法复杂度与连贯性。
