# The New Chairs - B3

Vikram walks into the café and sees Lin surrounded by wooden parts and **screws**.

- **Vikram**: Lin, what is all this?
- **Lin**: New chairs. I got them on sale. Cheap price, and they're DIY!
- **Vikram**: DIY? You mean you have to **build** them?
- **Lin**: Yeah. It's fun, isn't it? Don't worry, I **got** this.

Lin tightens a screw, but the chair **wobbles**.

- **Vikram**: Are you sure about this? These chairs need to be strong for our customers.
- **Lin**: Relax, Vikram. I followed the instructions. Mostly.
- **Vikram**: Lin, the instructions are important! They are **precise** for a reason.
- **Lin**: It's fine! I **tied some extra knots** to keep things together.

Later, the café opens, and a customer sits on one of the new chairs.

The chair collapses, and the customer falls to the floor.

- **Vikram**: Oh no! Are you okay?

The customer angrily **demands** a refund and leaves.

- **Vikram**: Lin, what happened?
- **Lin**: Maybe these chairs weren't the best idea.
- **Vikram**: I explained earlier that good chairs are an investment! Now we're losing money.
- **Lin**: Well, at least we saved on the chairs.
- **Vikram**: Lin, this isn't saving. This is **disaster management**!
- **Lin**: Okay, next time, I'll buy chairs that don't, you know, collapse.
- **Vikram**: Next time?!
- **Lin**: Yeah, assuming we still have customers.

## Vocabulary

- wobble: 英 /'wɒbl/, 美 /'wɑːbl/
  - vi. 摇晃；摇摆；游移不定
  - n. 摆动；不稳定；摇晃
  - vt. 使摇摆；使颤动；摇动
- precise: 英 /prɪ'saɪs/, 美 /prɪ'saɪs/
  - adj. 精确的；明确的；严格的

## Chinese

新椅子

Vikram 走进咖啡馆，看到 Lin 正被木制零件和螺丝包围着。

- **Vikram**：Lin，这都是些什么？
- **Lin**：新椅子。我打折买的。价格便宜，而且是 DIY！
- **Vikram**：DIY？意思是你得自己组装？
- **Lin**：是啊。这很有趣，不是吗？别担心，我**搞得定**。

Lin 拧紧了一颗螺丝，但椅子却**摇晃**起来。

- **Vikram**：你确定吗？这些椅子得足够结实才能让顾客使用。
- **Lin**：放轻松，Vikram。我照着说明书做的。大部分啦。
- **Vikram**：Lin，说明书很重要！它的精确是有原因的。
- **Lin**：没事！我额外打了几个结来固定。

后来，咖啡馆开门营业，一位顾客坐在其中一把新椅子上。

椅子塌了，顾客摔到地上。

- **Vikram**：糟了！你没事吧？

顾客愤怒地要求退款，然后离开了。

- **Vikram**：Lin，发生什么事了？
- **Lin**：也许这些椅子不是个好主意。
- **Vikram**：我之前就说过，好椅子是一种投资！现在我们反而在赔钱。
- **Lin**：好吧，但至少我们在椅子上省了钱。
- **Vikram**：Lin，这不叫省钱。这叫灾难管理！
- **Lin**：好吧，下次我会买不会塌的椅子。
- **Vikram**：下次？！
- **Lin**：是啊，前提是我们还会有顾客。

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**Why did the customer leave the café?**

The customer left the café because the chair collapsed as soon as he sat down. Naturally, he was shocked and upset, so he demanded a refund right away. From his point of view, it must have felt very **unprofessional** and even **unsafe** to stay any longer. I think most people would react in the same way, because when you visit a café, you expect comfort and safety, not broken furniture. That’s why he decided to leave.

顾客离开咖啡馆是因为他一坐下椅子就塌了。很自然，他感到震惊和生气，所以立刻要求退款。从他的角度来看，继续留下来一定觉得既不专业又不安全。我认为大多数人都会有同样的反应，因为去咖啡馆时，人们期待的是舒适和安全，而不是坏掉的家具。这就是他选择离开的原因。

> Explanation:

This version reaches Band 7+ for Speaking because it:

- Extends the idea naturally (not just one sentence).
- Uses everyday connectors (“so,” “because,” “that’s why”).
- Shows awareness of the customer’s perspective (adds depth).
- Uses natural spoken expressions (“Naturally,” “from his point of view”).

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

**Why did the customer leave the café?**

The customer left the café because the chair collapsed immediately after he sat down, which created both **embarrassment** and **frustration**. **Understandably**, he demanded a refund before leaving, as the incident suggested that the café had not **prioritized** safety or quality. From a business perspective, such situations can damage a café’s reputation, since customers **generally** expect reliability and comfort in a public space. Therefore, the customer’s decision to leave was not only **reasonable** but also **predictable**, given the circumstances. The event highlights how poor investment in furniture can lead to **financial loss** and **customer dissatisfaction**.

顾客离开咖啡馆是因为他一坐下椅子就立刻塌了，这让他既尴尬又沮丧。可以理解的是，他在离开前要求了退款，因为这一事件表明咖啡馆没有重视安全或质量。从商业角度来看，这种情况会损害咖啡馆的声誉，因为顾客通常在公共场所期望可靠性和舒适性。因此，顾客选择离开不仅合理，而且在这种情况下是可以预见的。这一事件凸显了在家具上投资不足如何导致财务损失和顾客不满。

> Explanation:

This version reaches Band 7+ for Writing because it:

- Uses complex sentences with correct punctuation (e.g., “..., which created both embarrassment and frustration”).
- Employs formal vocabulary (e.g., “understandably,” “prioritized,” “dissatisfaction”).
- Provides analysis beyond the event (reputation, business perspective).
- Maintains logical cohesion with connectors (“understandably,” “from a business perspective,” “therefore”).
