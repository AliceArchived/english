# The Art Auction - 2 - B3

Lucy and Oscar enter a large, fancy gala. People are dressed in elegant clothes, and paintings are displayed everywhere.

- **Oscar**: **Medalists** who paint? This is what the gala is for? Lucy, their work is just… **decorative fluff**.
- **Lucy**: Perhaps, but people love their stories. Maybe you could learn something.
- **Oscar**: Learn? From them? Lucy, good art should inspire. Their art is just for show.
- **Lucy**: Oh, really? And who decides what is 'good art,' Oscar? You?
- **Oscar**: Yes! I'm the one who knows! Trust me, Lucy, I'd never make art just to please an audience.

Lucy **smirks** but doesn't answer. They walk further into the gala.

At the center of the room, an auction begins. A painting is revealed — a **striking** unsigned piece.

- **Oscar**: Wait… that's my painting. I donated it years ago to a **fundraiser**!
- **Lucy**: Are you sure?
- **Oscar**: Of course! Don't you recognize my brushstrokes? It has to be mine!

The auctioneer announces the bidding will start at $10,000. The crowd gasps. Quickly, **the bids climb**.

$10,000 becomes $20,000, then $50,000. Finally, the painting sells for $100,000.

- **Oscar**: One hundred thousand?! For my unsigned painting?! I must be a genius!
- **Lucy**: Perhaps it's the story behind the painting they love, not just the art.
- **Oscar**: **Nonsense**, Lucy. This proves I'm worth more than these medalists.
- **Lucy**: **If you say so,** Oscar. **Just don't let it go to your head.**
- **Oscar**: Too late! I've already decided — I'm breaking records now!

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-26

**What surprised Oscar at the auction?**

What really surprised Oscar **at the auction** was that one of his unsigned paintings, which he had donated to a fundraiser years earlier, was sold for **an astonishing** one hundred thousand dollars. He never expected that a painting without his name on it could **attract** so much attention or **reach** such a high price. The moment made him feel proud, and he immediately started to **reconsider** his own value as an artist, almost seeing himself as **a record-breaking genius**.

在拍卖会上真正让奥斯卡吃惊的是，他几年前捐给筹款活动的一幅未署名画作竟然以十万美元的天价售出。他从未想到，一幅没有签名的画也能吸引如此多的关注，并达到这么高的价格。这个时刻让他倍感自豪，他立刻开始重新思考自己作为艺术家的价值，几乎把自己看成是一位打破纪录的天才。

> Explanation:

This version fits IELTS Speaking Band 7+ because:

- **Fluency and naturalness** – Uses conversational openings like _What really surprised Oscar was…_ and narrative flow suitable for speaking.
- **Clarity and cohesion** – Ideas are linked smoothly with connectors (_suddenly_, _never expected_, _the moment made him_).
- **Emotional engagement** – Includes expressive words like _astonishing_, _proud_, _record-breaking genius_, showing natural spoken emphasis.
- **Appropriate complexity** – Sentences are varied in length, combining simple and complex clauses without sounding overly formal.
- **Length and substance** – At around 90 words, it’s detailed enough for a full IELTS speaking response while still easy to say aloud.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-26

**What surprised Oscar at the auction?**

At the auction, Oscar was **astonished** to discover that one of his unsigned paintings, which he had donated to a fundraiser many years ago, **unexpectedly appeared on display**. What **shocked** him most was that **this work**, **without a signature**, was sold for **an extraordinary sum** of one hundred thousand dollars. He had never imagined that a piece he **had casually donated without his name on it** could reach such a high value. This **surprising outcome** led him to **reconsider** his worth as an artist. In fact, he proudly **concluded** that he was more talented than the celebrated medalists and even capable of breaking records in the art world.

在拍卖会上，奥斯卡惊讶地发现，他多年前捐赠给一次筹款活动的一幅未署名画作竟然再次出现。最令他震惊的是，这幅没有签名的作品竟以十万美元的高价售出。他从未想过，一幅自己随意捐出的、没有署名的作品能够达到如此的价值。这一意外的结果促使他重新审视自己作为艺术家的价值。事实上，他自豪地认为自己的天赋超越了那些获奖的画家，甚至有能力在艺术界创造新的纪录。

> Explanation:

This version fits IELTS Writing Band 7+ because:

1. **Accuracy** – It uses precise and context-appropriate wording like _unsigned_, _without a signature_, and _without his name on it_, which avoids the ambiguity of “without recognition.”
2. **Complex structures** – Sentences include subordinate clauses (“which he had donated…,” “he had never imagined that…”) and cause-effect links (“This surprising outcome led him to reconsider…”).
3. **Cohesion and coherence** – Logical connectors (_what shocked him most_, _in fact_) provide smooth transitions.
4. **Formal tone** – Vocabulary such as _astonished_, _extraordinary sum_, _proudly concluded_ elevates the academic register.
5. **Length and depth** – At ~120 words, it develops the idea fully without redundancy, demonstrating strong control of written discourse.

### Practice History

#### 2025-08-26

**What surprised Oscar at the auction?**

Helpful Phrases: Oscar was/ unsigned/ sold for

Oscar found one of his unsigned ~~painting~~ was sold ~~in~~ a surprisingly high price — one hundred thousand dollars — which was really unexpected — either the appearance of his paintings or the price it was sold. The painting was donated to a fundraiser years ago. This surprise made Oscar ~~felt~~ obliged to revalue himself.

> Corrections:

1. one of his unsigned painting” → should be plural “paintings.”
2. “was sold in a surprisingly high price” → correct collocation is “sold for a surprisingly high price.”
3. “made Oscar felt” → correct form is “made Oscar **feel**.”
4. Smoothed awkward phrase “either the appearance… or the price” → “**both in terms of the painting’s appearance and the price it reached.**”

Oscar found that one of his **unsigned paintings** was sold **for** a surprisingly high price — one hundred thousand dollars — which was really unexpected, both **in terms of** the painting’s appearance and the price it reached. He had donated the painting to a fundraiser years ago. This surprise made Oscar **feel** obliged to revalue himself.

奥斯卡发现他的一幅未署名的画以惊人的高价——十万美元——被拍卖，这对他来说非常意外，不论是画作的外观还是它最终的成交价。他几年前把这幅画捐给了一场筹款活动。这一惊喜让奥斯卡觉得必须重新评价自己。
