# Moving in with Violet - B3

Lin sits on the living room floor, **scrolling through her phone**. Lucy walks in, carrying a cup of tea.

- **Lucy**: What are you **up to**, Lin?
- **Lin**: Oh, just checking apartments. I found a cool new place.
- **Lucy**: A new place? You're moving out?
- **Lin**: Yeah, I'm moving in with **a paranormal investigator**. She's charming and has a great mustache.
- **Lucy**: A mustache? Are you serious?
- **Lin**: Yep. She has a British accent too.
- **Lucy**: A British accent? I shouldn't take her seriously, should I?
- **Lin**: Her name is Violet. We met online.
- **Lucy**: That's it — I need to meet this Violet.
- **Lin**: Why?
- **Lucy**: To make sure she's not a **fraud**! Or worse… a spy.
- **Lin**: Grandma, she solves paranormal mysteries, not crimes.
- **Lucy**: Hah! That's just a cover. What if she's my old enemy from **Morocco**?
- **Lin**: Morocco?
- **Lucy**: Never mind. **Forget I said anything**.

The doorbell rings. Lin stands up and heads to the door. Lucy follows.

- **Lucy**: Wait! Don't open the door yet.
- **Lin**: Why not?
- **Lucy**: What if it's a trap?

Lin opens the door. It's the pizza delivery person.

- **Lin**: It's just pizza, Grandma.
- **Lucy**: Oh. Well, I'm still not **convinced about** Violet!
- **Lin**: Don't worry, Grandma. I'll let you meet her before I move… but can we eat first?
- **Lucy**: Fine. But if she has a worse mustache than me, she's not moving in with you!

## Vocabulary

- violet: 英 /'vaɪələt/, 美 /'vaɪələt/
  - n. 紫罗兰
- paranormal: 英 /ˌpærə'nɔːml/, 美 /ˌpærə'nɔːrml/
  - adj. 超出科学可知范围的,不正常的, 奇异的

## Chinese

- **Lucy**：Lin，你在干什么？
- **Lin**：哦，只是在看公寓。我找到一个很酷的新地方。

- **Lin**：是的，我要和一位**超自然调查员**同住。她很有魅力，还留着一撮大胡子。
- **Lucy**：胡子？你是认真的吗？
- **Lin**：嗯哼。她还有英国口音。
- **Lucy**：英国口音？我不该当真，对吧？

- **Lin**：为什么？

## Style

- Changed "Lucy: Fine. But if she has a **worse mustache** than me, she's not moving in with you!" → "Lucy: Fine. But if she has a bigger mustache than me, she's not moving in with you!" (since "worse mustache" is grammatically awkward — "bigger" fits better here)

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

**Why does Lucy want to meet Violet?**

Lucy wants to meet Violet because she feels something is **not quite right about her**. Lin has decided to **move in with** Violet, but Lucy **suspects** that Violet could **even** be a spy. From Lin’s description, Violet sounds rather unusual — she has a mustache, a British accent, and works as a paranormal investigator. All of this makes Lucy feel **uneasy**, so she insists on meeting Violet **in person** before allowing Lin to make such a big decision.

Lucy 想见 Violet，因为她觉得 Violet 有些不对劲。Lin 决定和 Violet 一起住，但 Lucy 怀疑 Violet 甚至可能是个间谍。根据 Lin 的描述，Violet 听起来相当特别——她留着小胡子，说着英国口音，而且还是一名超自然调查员。所有这些都让 Lucy 感到不安，因此她坚持要亲自见见 Violet，才会允许 Lin 做出这样的重要决定。

> Explanation:

This version fits **IELTS Speaking Band 7+** because it:

- Uses natural spoken connectors (“because,” “but,” “so”).
- Expands ideas with examples (“mustache, a British accent, and works as a paranormal investigator”).
- Shows fluency and coherence with logical flow.
- Contains enough lexical variety (“suspects,” “unusual,” “uneasy,” “decision”).

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

**Why does Lucy want to meet Violet?**

Lucy wants to meet Violet mainly **out of concern** for Lin’s safety. Since Lin plans to move out and live with Violet, Lucy believes it is necessary to verify who Violet really is. Based on Lin’s description, Violet appears **suspicious**. She is **described** as a woman with a mustache, a British accent, and a rather unusual **occupation** as a paranormal investigator. These **characteristics** make Lucy think Violet could even be a spy. Therefore, before Lin **finalizes** her decision to move in, Lucy insists on meeting Violet personally to ensure that her granddaughter is making **a safe and sensible choice**.

Lucy 想见 Violet，主要是出于对 Lin 安全的担忧。由于 Lin 计划搬出去与 Violet 同住，Lucy 认为有必要核实 Violet 的真实身份。根据 Lin 的描述，Violet 看起来很可疑。她被描绘成一个长着小胡子、带着英国口音、而且有着超自然调查员这种不同寻常职业的女人。这些特征让 Lucy 怀疑 Violet 甚至可能是个间谍。因此，在 Lin 最终决定搬入之前，Lucy 坚持要亲自见一见 Violet，以确保她的外孙女做出的是一个安全且明智的选择。

> Explanation:

This version fits **IELTS Writing Band 7+** because it:

- Uses formal, academic-style connectors (“mainly out of concern,” “since,” “therefore”).
- Develops ideas logically with clear cause–effect reasoning.
- Includes precise vocabulary (“verify,” “characteristics,” “finalizes,” “sensible choice”).
- Has complex sentence structures with subordination and relative clauses.
- Length is appropriate for Task 2 style responses.

### Practice History

**Why does Lucy want to meet Violet?**

Helpful Phrases: Lucy wants/ to make / a spy

Lucy wants to meet Violet to make sure she is not a spy because Lin wants to move out and live with this woman. According to Lin's description, Violet is suspicious, a woman with mustache and ~~work~~ as paranormal investigator.

> Corrections:

1. “work as” → corrected to “works as” (subject–verb agreement).
2. Added a comma after “spy” for clarity.
3. Changed “this woman” → “her” (more natural and less repetitive).
4. Smoothed phrasing: “is suspicious, a woman with mustache” → “seems suspicious — she is a woman with a mustache”.
5. Adjusted tense and flow to sound more natural.

Lucy wants to meet Violet to make sure she is not a spy, because Lin **plans** to move out and live with her. According to Lin’s description, Violet **seems** suspicious — she is a woman with a mustache and **works** as a paranormal investigator.

Lucy 想见 Violet，是为了确认她不是间谍，因为 Lin 打算搬出去和她同住。根据 Lin 的描述，Violet 看起来很可疑——她是个长着小胡子的女人，还从事超自然调查的工作。
