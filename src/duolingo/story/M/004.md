# Meditation App Drama - B3

Bea is excited about her new meditation app. She wants Lin to record **relaxing guided sessions**.

- **Bea**: Lin, just read these scripts. They're **soothing** and calm. Perfect for the app!
- **Lin**: Sure. Relaxing is my thing.

Lin takes the scripts and heads into the recording room. Bea **checks on** her a while later.

- **Bea**: How's it going?
- **Lin**: Great! But, uh… I didn't use your scripts. I had some thoughts of my own.
- **Bea**: What? Lin, you can't just **make stuff up**! This app is about peace, calm, order!
- **Lin**: Yeah, I get it. But apparently, people like **honesty**. My way is honest.

Bea grabs the headphones and listens to Lin's recordings.

- **Lin**: "Why do we stress so much about Mondays? Life's short. Eat a sandwich. Take a nap. Mondays aren't that bad."
- **Bea**: Lin! This isn't relaxing. This is… weird.
- **Lin**: I don't know. People might like it.
- **Bea**: I doubt it. I need to delete these before anyone hears them.

Just then, Bea gets a notification.

- **Bea**: Wait… someone already reviewed it?

She clicks the app.

- **Bea**: "Loved Lin's sessions. So real. So funny. Five stars"?
- **Lin**: Huh. Told you.
- **Bea**: But… this wasn't the impression I wanted for the app!
- **Lin**: Maybe, but it's the impression people want. So, should I record more?
- **Bea**: Fine. Just don't record anything about sandwiches again.
- **Lin**: No promises.

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-23

**Why did Bea check Lin's recordings?**

Bea decided to check Lin’s recordings because she had prepared the scripts herself and wanted to ensure that Lin was reading them exactly as planned. **From her point of view**, the app was supposed to create a sense of calm and order, so accuracy mattered a lot. However, Lin chose to ignore the scripts and spoke freely about his own thoughts. That contrast really shows why Bea felt the need to double-check — she wanted control, but Lin valued **honesty** and **spontaneity**.

Bea 决定检查 Lin 的录音，因为这些稿子是她亲自准备的，她想确保 Lin 完全按照计划来朗读。在她看来，这个应用本该营造一种平静和秩序的氛围，所以准确性非常重要。然而，Lin 却选择无视稿子，自由地表达自己的想法。正是这种反差说明了 Bea 为什么要去检查——她想要掌控，而 Lin 更看重真实与自发性。

> Explanation:

This version reaches Band 7+ because it:

- Expands the idea into a clear and coherent explanation.
- Uses connectors (“because,” “so,” “however”) for fluency.
- Balances description with interpretation.
- Contains variety in sentence length and some higher-level vocabulary (“spontaneity,” “contrast,” “accuracy”).
- Natural, conversational tone suitable for Speaking.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-23

**Why did Bea check Lin's recordings?**

Bea checked Lin’s recordings because she had written the scripts herself and wanted to ensure that he **followed** them exactly. From her **perspective**, **the meditation app** was intended to **reflect** peace, calm, and order, which meant that every word in the script carried importance. By verifying the recordings, Bea was trying to maintain consistency and protect **the image of the app**. However, the situation **reveals** an interesting **tension**: while Bea prioritized **structure and discipline**, Lin preferred to emphasize honesty and humor. This **clash** explains why Bea felt **compelled** to review her work carefully.

Bea 检查 Lin 的录音，是因为这些稿子是她亲自写的，她想确保他能完全按照稿子来读。对 Bea 来说，这个冥想应用应当体现宁静、平和和秩序，因此稿子里的每个字都很重要。通过检查录音，Bea 试图保持一致性，并维护应用的形象。然而，这种情况也揭示了一种有趣的张力：Bea 更重视结构和规范，而 Lin 则倾向于强调真实与幽默。正因如此，Bea 才感到有必要认真审查他的工作。

> Explanation:

This version is Band 7+ in Writing because it:

- Provides clear reasoning in a formal, academic tone.
- Uses precise vocabulary (“maintain consistency,” “protect the image,” “reveals a tension”).
- Develops the answer into a well-structured paragraph with cause–effect and contrast.
- Demonstrates lexical variety and coherence — hallmarks of Band 7+.

### Practice History

#### 2025-08-23

**Why did Bea check Lin's recordings?**

Helpful Phrases: Bea checked/ because/ the scripts

Bea checked Lin's recordings because she wrote the scripts and wanted to make sure Lin followed the instructions. Again, Lin didn't follow them.

> Corrections:

1. Changed “she wrote” → “she had written” (past perfect fits better since the script-writing happened before the checking).
2. Replaced “Again” with “However” for clearer logical contrast.

Bea checked Lin's recordings because she **had written** the scripts and wanted to make sure Lin followed her instructions. **However**, Lin didn’t follow them.

Bea 之所以检查 Lin 的录音，是因为她写了这些稿子，并且想确认 Lin 是否遵循了她的指示。然而，Lin 并没有遵循。
