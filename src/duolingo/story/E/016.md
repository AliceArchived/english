# Entertaining Kids and Dates - B3

Eddy's Advice Column

Hello, friends,

I'm Eddy. And today, the question is 'How can I **keep** both my kids and my dates **entertained**?' You might say it's impossible, but I have some ideas! Here's what you can do:

**Plan a Fun Activity**

Do something everyone enjoys! Go for an outdoor hike or set up a game night. Not only is it fun, but it also **boosts everybody's mood**! Junior loves games, and if there's a chocolate **prize**, he'll play until he wins!

**Show Off Your Cooking Skills**

Cook something **tasty** for everybody. Not only can you impress your date, but your kids will love it too! Junior and I made a pizza last week. It was a big hit! We all had more confidence in our cooking skills afterward!

**Be the Funniest**

Make everyone laugh! Tell jokes or funny stories. Both kids and dates love someone with a sense of humor. I swear, I'm not the funniest, but Junior thinks I'm **hilarious**. It's a great way to keep both your date and your kids entertained!

With these tips, you can impress your date and keep your kids entertained!

Stay strong!
Eddy

**计划一个有趣的活动**

**展示你的厨艺**

为大家做一些美味的食物。这不仅能打动你的约会对象，你的孩子们也会喜欢！上周我和 Junior 一起做了一个披萨。结果大受欢迎！之后我们对自己的厨艺更有信心了！

**成为最搞笑的人**

让大家都笑起来！讲笑话或有趣的故事。孩子和约会对象都喜欢有幽默感的人。我发誓，我不是最搞笑的，但 Junior 觉得我很滑稽。这是让孩子和约会对象都开心的好办法！

有了这些技巧，你既能打动你的约会对象，又能让孩子们玩得开心！

保持坚强！  
Eddy

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-09-04

**Which of Eddy's tips do you like most?**

I think **the best tip** is the Fun Activity one because it can be **applied instantly** and it **works well in different situations**. Unlike cooking or trying to be the funniest, which **require more preparation or effort**, a simple activity such as hiking, playing games, or even going for a walk can **create a cheerful atmosphere** right away. It helps both kids and dates **bond naturally**, almost like an icebreaker, and afterward you can **gradually introduce the other tips** into daily life.

我认为最好的建议是“有趣活动”，因为它可以立刻应用，并且在不同的场合都很适用。相比于做饭或者努力搞笑，这些需要更多准备或精力，而简单的活动，比如远足、玩游戏，甚至散步，都能立刻营造一种愉快的氛围。它能让孩子和约会对象自然地产生联系，几乎就像一种破冰方式。之后，你还可以逐渐把其他建议融入日常生活。

> Explanation:

This version fits Band 7+ for Speaking because it:

- Uses natural, spoken-style connectors (“because,” “unlike,” “such as,” “almost like”).
- Has clear organization (contrast + example + conclusion).
- Vocabulary is varied but not too formal (“instantly,” “bond naturally,” “cheerful atmosphere”).
- Tone is conversational, showing fluency.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-09-04

**Which of Eddy's tips do you like most?**

Among Eddy’s tips, I believe **the most practical one** is the Fun Activity suggestion. The reason is that it can be **implemented immediately** and has **an instant positive effect**. Unlike cooking or telling jokes, which require preparation, planning a simple activity such as going on a short hike, arranging a game night, or even taking a walk can **bring people together very quickly**. It creates opportunities for both children and dates to **share enjoyable experiences**, functioning as an effective icebreaker. After this **initial bonding**, the other two tips can be gradually **incorporated into** daily life to strengthen relationships further.

在 Eddy 的建议中，我认为最实用的是“有趣活动”。原因在于它可以立即实施，并能立刻产生积极效果。相比之下，做饭或讲笑话都需要准备，而计划一项简单的活动，比如短途远足、组织游戏之夜，甚至散步，都能很快把人们聚在一起。这能让孩子和约会对象共享愉快的体验，起到有效的破冰作用。经过这种初步的联系之后，其余两个建议也可以逐渐融入日常生活，从而进一步加强关系。

> Explanation:

This version fits Band 7+ for Writing because it:

- Uses academic-style linking (“the reason is that,” “unlike,” “functioning as,” “after this initial bonding”).
- Has precise vocabulary (“implemented,” “instant positive effect,” “incorporated into daily life”).
- Structured clearly with logical development (reason → contrast → examples → conclusion).
- Formal tone appropriate for IELTS Writing Task 2 style.
