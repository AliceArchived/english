# Junior's Scenic Maps - B3

Eddy walks into the living room and sees Junior drawing on a big map.

- **Eddy**: Hey, Junior. What are you working on?
- **Junior**: Dad, I've started a new business! It's called "Junior's Amazing Tours!"
- **Eddy**: Oh no. What is "Junior's Amazing Tours"?
- **Junior**: I sell maps to **the best scenic spots** in the area. **Tourism** is very popular, Dad!
- **Eddy**: That's… creative. But where are these spots?
- **Junior**: I marked a few cool places. Look, here's a hill, here's an old building…
- **Eddy**: Junior, this map says "Cool Treehouse" on Mr. Harris's backyard.
- **Junior**: Yeah! It's a great treehouse.
- **Eddy**: People can't go to Mr. Harris's backyard! That's private property!
  - Wait, Junior. Isn't this Mrs. Green's garden?
- **Junior**: Um, maybe. But her flowers are the best **scenery**!

Eddy notices another spot marked "Perfect Picnic Place."

Later, angry neighbors knock on Eddy's door.

- **Eddy**: Hi, everyone! I'm so, so sorry about this! I'll handle it!

Eddy spends the day **making apologies** and **returning unhappy tourists to their cars**.

That night, Junior sits on the couch with a notebook.

- **Eddy**: What are you doing now?
- **Junior**: Counting my money! I earned $15 today.
- **Eddy**: But you caused so much trouble!
- **Junior**: Trouble? No way? People bought my maps, so the business was a success!
- **Eddy**: Junior, remind me to sign you up for a "tourism rules" class next summer.
- **Junior**: Only if there's ice cream after class.

## Vocabulary

- tourism: 英 /'tʊərɪzəm/, 美 /'tʊrɪzəm/
  - n. 旅游业；游览

## Speaking & Writing

### 🌟 Speaking Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-20

**Why were the neighbors upset about Junior's maps?**

Well, the neighbors were really upset about Junior’s maps because they actually **directed** strangers **into** private areas, like someone’s backyard or garden. From their perspective, it was a big **invasion** of privacy. Imagine if people suddenly showed up in your garden just because they bought a map—that would be annoying, right? So I think the neighbors felt **disrespected** and probably worried about safety. That’s why they complained and came to Eddy’s house.

嗯，邻居们对 Junior 的地图非常不满，因为那些地图实际上把陌生人引到了私人区域，比如别人的后院或花园。从他们的角度来看，这是一种很大的隐私侵犯。想象一下，如果有人因为买了一张地图就突然跑到你的花园里，你肯定会很烦，对吧？所以我认为邻居们觉得不被尊重，而且可能还担心安全问题。这就是他们抱怨并来到 Eddy 家的原因。

> Explanation:

This version uses natural spoken style with fillers (“Well,” “actually,” “imagine if…”), rhetorical question, and emotional reaction, which matches IELTS Speaking Band 7+. It’s coherent, has extended sentences, and uses a variety of structures.

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-20

**Why were the neighbors upset about Junior's maps?**

The neighbors were upset about Junior’s maps because they **unintentionally** encouraged people to enter private property. Several of the **scenic spots** Junior highlighted were not public places but rather **personal spaces**, such as Mr. Harris’s backyard and Mrs. Green’s garden. From the neighbors’ perspective, this behavior was **highly inappropriate**, as it ignored basic respect for privacy and property rights. Their frustration grew when unexpected visitors began showing up at their homes, disrupting their daily lives. Consequently, they felt **compelled** to confront Eddy and demand an explanation. This **illustrates** how Junior’s business idea caused significant community tension.

邻居们对 Junior 的地图感到不满，因为这些地图无意中鼓励人们进入私人领地。Junior 标记的一些风景点并不是公共场所，而是私人空间，比如 Harris 先生的后院和 Green 太太的花园。从邻居的角度来看，这种行为非常不合适，因为它忽视了对隐私和产权的基本尊重。当意料之外的访客开始出现在他们家时，他们的沮丧情绪加剧，打乱了他们的日常生活。因此，他们感到必须与 Eddy 当面对质并要求解释。这表明 Junior 的商业想法引发了严重的社区矛盾。

> Explanation:

This version is more formal and structured, suitable for Writing Band 7+. It uses clear connectors (“because,” “as,” “consequently”), precise vocabulary (“inappropriate,” “disrupting,” “compelled”), and logical progression. The argument is well-developed and exceeds 100 words, fulfilling IELTS Writing requirements.

### Practice History

#### 2025-08-20

**Why were the neighbors upset about Junior's maps?**

Helpful Phrases: The neighbors/ sent people/ private property

The neighbors were upset about Junior's map because it sent people to their private property.

> Corrections:

1. “map” → should be plural “maps” (the story shows Junior made multiple marked spots). Also, “it sent people” → should be “they sent people,” because “maps” is plural.

The neighbors were upset about Junior's **maps** because **they** sent people to their private property.

邻居们对 Junior 的地图感到不满，因为那些地图把人引到了他们的私人领地上。
