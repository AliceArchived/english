# Winning an Art Campaign - B3

A chapter from "Living Like an Artist"

As an artist, I know that **promoting** oneself is necessary. Campaigns for the best art are no different. So, are you ready to learn from the best? That's me, of course! Here is how to ensure your art campaign succeeds:

**Start by telling everyone you're the greatest candidate.**

Tell everyone that you are the greatest artist candidate there could ever be. Say, "Art is important! Support my campaign!" You'll gain many votes this way.

**Create art that opponents can't compete with.**

Paintings, sculptures, or even a modern art installation — make art that is unforgettable and causes opponents to wonder why they even try. Your art will be so good that people won't even look at the opponent's work.

**Use unusual campaign strategies.**

Organize art shows in unusual places like fitting rooms or the local zoo. This surprising strategy will help people remember you. Plus, they'll talk about how creative you are in their circles.

Remember, in the world of art campaigns, you're not just an artist — you're a candidate to be admired. Your work deserves the spotlight!

Now you can live like me!

Congratulations.

**Which of Oscar's tips is most creative?**

In my opinion, the most creative tip is **definitely** the one **about** using unusual campaign strategies. **The reason is that** telling everyone you are the best candidate is just **a standard thing** to do, and it doesn’t really stand out. Also, creating art that opponents cannot compete with **sounds impressive**, but **in reality** it is extremely difficult, so not many candidates could actually **achieve** it. 在我看来，最有创意的建议无疑是采用不同寻常的竞选策略。原因是，告诉大家你是最优秀的候选人只是一个常规动作，并不能真正让人印象深刻。而创造出对手完全无法竞争的艺术作品听起来很厉害，但实际上非常困难，很少有人能够真正做到。相比之下，采用出人意料或令人惊讶的策略，比如在特别的场所举办艺术展，既切实可行又令人难忘，因此这是最有创意的建议。

### 🌟 Writing Band 7.0+

#### 2025-08-26

**Which of Oscar's tips is most creative?**

In my opinion, the most creative suggestion from the passage is the idea of **adopting** unusual campaign strategies. The first piece of advice, which is to tell everyone that you are the greatest candidate, seems rather **ordinary** because it is **essentially** a routine action that all candidates are expected to perform. The second suggestion, to create art that opponents cannot compete with, may sound **inspiring**, but in reality it is highly **impractical** since very few people are **capable** of producing such extraordinary work. In contrast, **making use of** unusual and surprising strategies, such as organizing art exhibitions in unexpected places, is both **feasible and original**. For this reason, I believe this is the most **creative** and **effective** tip.

在我看来，这篇文章中最有创意的建议是采用不同寻常的竞选策略。第一个建议，即告诉大家你是最伟大的候选人，看起来相当普通，因为这实际上是所有候选人都必须做的例行公事。第二个建议是创作出对手无法竞争的艺术作品，这听起来虽然鼓舞人心，但实际上非常不切实际，因为极少有人能够创作出如此非凡的作品。相比之下，采取不寻常且令人惊喜的策略，比如在意想不到的地方举办艺术展览，既**可行又新颖**。因此，我认为这是最具创意且最有效的建议。

> Explanation:

This version reaches Band 7+ for Writing because:

- Formal academic tone (“suggestion,” “in reality,” “impractical,” “feasible and original”).
- Logical structure with clear connectors (“The first… The second… In contrast…”).
- Sophisticated vocabulary and collocations: “extraordinary work,” “adopting unusual campaign strategies,” “for this reason.”
- Developed argument with analysis rather than just opinion.
- Coherent paragraph with strong conclusion.
