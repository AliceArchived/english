# Lesson 1 - Sign Shapes and Colours

Navigating the road safely requires understanding traffic signs, which serve as vital guides about laws, alert us to hazards, and aid in reaching our destinations.

标志的形状与颜色

安全驾驶需要理解交通标志。交通标志是重要的指引，它们告诉我们法律规定、提醒我们潜在危险，并帮助我们到达目的地。

As mentioned before, there will be 20 questions on traffic signs in the G1 test. A passing score means getting at least 16 of these questions right. To prepare you for this, this chapter goes through the essential traffic signs you should be familiar with for the test, and breaks them down into these lessons:

- Common Signs
- Permissive Signs
- Prohibitory Signs
- Regulatory Signs
- Warning Signs
- Construction Signs
- Direction & Information Signs

如前所述，在 G1 笔试中会有 **20 道关于交通标志的题目**。要通过考试，至少要答对其中 **16 道**。为了帮助你备考，本章将带你学习需要熟悉的核心交通标志，并将它们分为以下几个类别：

- 常见标志
- 允许性标志
- 禁止性标志
- 管制性标志
- 警告性标志
- 施工标志
- 方向与信息标志

Designed for easy recognition, these signs incorporate distinctive symbols, colours, and shapes. Regulatory traffic signs, for instance, use specific words or symbols to indicate what is allowed or forbidden in given scenarios.

这些标志通过独特的 **图形、颜色和形状** 设计，便于快速识别。  
例如，管制性标志（Regulatory Signs）会使用特定的文字或符号，来说明在某种情况下 **什么是允许的，什么是禁止的**。

> **Take note**:
> the **shape** and **colour** of a traffic sign are chosen based on the message it's intended to convey. To bolster your understanding, here's an overview of key traffic signs and their meanings:

> **注意**：  
> 每一个交通标志的 **形状** 和 **颜色**，都是根据其所要传达的信息来设计的。

G1 Traffic Sign Shapes & Colours

（安省 G1 笔试交通标志形状与颜色）

1. Shapes（形状）

| **Shape**                | **English Meaning**                 | **中文含义**                            |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Octagon (8-sided)**    | **STOP** sign only                  | 八边形 → 专用于 **停车标志**            |
| **Upside-down Triangle** | **YIELD**                           | 倒三角形 → **让路标志**                 |
| **Circle**               | **Railway crossing warning**        | 圆形 → **铁路道口警告**                 |
| **Diamond**              | **Warning** (hazards, curves, etc.) | 菱形 → **警告标志**（弯道、危险、路况） |
| **Rectangle / Square**   | **Regulatory / Information**        | 矩形或正方形 → **规定或信息标志**       |
| **Pentagon (5-sided)**   | **School zone / school crossing**   | 五边形 → **学校区域 / 学生过街**        |

2. Colours（颜色）

| **Colour**        | **English Meaning**                        | **中文含义**                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| **Red**           | Stop / Prohibition                         | 红色 → **停车 / 禁止**                  |
| **Green**         | Permissive (directions, allowed movements) | 绿色 → **允许 / 方向指引**              |
| **Yellow**        | General warning                            | 黄色 → **一般警告**                     |
| **Orange**        | Construction / Temporary condition         | 橙色 → **施工 / 临时情况**              |
| **Blue**          | Road user services / Tourist info          | 蓝色 → **服务信息 / 旅游指引**          |
| **Brown**         | Parks / Recreation                         | 棕色 → **公园 / 娱乐区**                |
| **Black & White** | Regulatory (laws, speed limits, etc.)      | 黑白色 → **规章标志**（限速、车道规则） |

📘 **考试口诀**：

- **八边形必停，倒三角让行，菱形警告，圆形铁路，五边学区，矩形规定。**
- **红停绿行，黄警橙工，蓝服务，棕旅游，黑白规章。**

中英解释

- **English Explanation**:  
   Traffic signs in Ontario are categorized by **shape** and **colour** to make them easily recognizable. For example, a **red circle with a slash on a white background** means “the action shown inside the circle is not permitted.” Shapes also matter — for instance, square-shaped signs are often used for regulatory or prohibitory instructions.
- **中文解释**：  
   安省的交通标志通过 **形状** 和 **颜色** 来帮助司机快速识别。比如：**白底红圈加斜杠** 表示“圈内的行为禁止”。标志的形状也很重要，例如 **正方形标志** 通常用于管制性或禁止性提示。
