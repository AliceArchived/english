# Lesson 2 - Common Signs

Here are some of the important signs you will see on Ontario roads:

## Stop Sign

![stop sign](/assets/images/life/drive/l2_stop.jpeg)

This sign requires you to come to a **complete** stop. Make sure to:

- Stop at the stop line if it's marked on the pavement.
- If there's no stop line, stop at the crosswalk.
- If there's no crosswalk, stop at the edge of the sidewalk.
- If there's no sidewalk, stop at the edge of the intersection and wait until it's clear and safe before entering the intersection.

## School Area

![stop sign](/assets/images/life/drive/l2_school_area.jpeg)

This sign indicates you're approaching a school zone. Be sure to slow down, drive with **extra caution**, and **watch for children** and **pedestrians**.

## Yield Sign

![yield sign](/assets/images/life/drive/l2_yield_sign.jpeg)

This sign means you must give the right of way to traffic in the intersection or near it. Stop if necessary and proceed only when it's clear.

Yield Sign（让路标志）这个标志表示你必须把行车优先权让给路口内或接近路口的其他车辆。必要时应当停车，并且只有在确认道路畅通、安全的情况下才能继续行驶。

The Yield sign (upside-down triangle, red border, white background) tells drivers that they must give the right of way to other vehicles already in or approaching the intersection. You don’t always need to stop, but you must slow down and stop if necessary until the way is clear.

Yield 标志（倒三角形，红边白底）要求司机在路口必须 让行，优先权属于已经在路口内或正在接近的车辆。通常不需要完全停车，但必须减速观察，若有必要则停车等待，直到道路安全才能通行。

## Railway Crossing Sign

![Railway Crossing Sign](/assets/images/life/drive/l2_railway_cross.jpeg)

This sign warns that railway tracks cross the road nearby. When you see this sign, slow down, **look both ways for trains**, and stay alert for stopping. We'll learn more about railway tracks in the following chapters.

Railway Crossing Sign（铁路道口标志）这个标志提示前方有铁路轨道穿过道路。看到此标志时，应减速，向两边观察是否有火车，并保持警觉，随时准备停车。在接下来的章节里，我们会进一步学习铁路道口的相关规则。

---

- The **Railway Crossing Sign** (round, yellow background, black “X” and “R R”) is a **warning sign**. It alerts drivers that train tracks are ahead. You must slow down, look in both directions for approaching trains, and be ready to stop if necessary.
- **铁路道口标志**（黄色圆形，黑色 “X” 和 “R R” 字样）属于 **警告标志**。它提示司机前方有火车轨道。看到该标志时，必须减速，左右观察是否有火车驶来，并随时准备停车。

Traffic signs in Ontario are grouped into four categories:

- **Regulatory signs** – indicate rules and laws (e.g., speed limits, no entry).
- **Warning signs** – alert drivers to potential hazards (e.g., curves, pedestrian crossings).
- **Construction signs** – inform drivers of temporary roadwork and conditions.
- **Direction & Information signs** – provide guidance such as route numbers, exits, or services.

在安省，交通标志分为 四大类：

- **管制标志** —— 表示法规和规则（如限速、禁止进入）。
- **警告标志** —— 提醒驾驶员潜在危险（如弯道、行人横道）。
- **施工标志** —— 通知驾驶员道路施工或临时状况。
- **方向与信息标志** —— 提供指引，如路线编号、出口、服务设施。
