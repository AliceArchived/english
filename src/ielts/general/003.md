## 🎯 使用技巧

1. **不要生硬套词**：替换时要保证 collocation（搭配自然）。
   - ❌ “increase the effectiveness of nature”
   - ✅ “increase the effectiveness of government policies”
2. **保持 variety**：同一篇文章中避免重复一个词超过 3 次。
3. **优先使用精确表达**：比如 “reduce efficiency” 可以写成 “undermine performance”，更 academic。

## 🎯 示例题目

> Some people think working from home makes employees more productive, while others believe it reduces efficiency. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### ✍️ Band 6.5 水平写法（词汇重复、表达普通）

> Some people think working from home improves productivity because workers do not need to **commute**, so they can save time and energy. However, other people believe it reduces efficiency, since employees may be distracted by personal matters. In my opinion, both views have truth, but for most jobs it reduces productivity, because people often spend time on non-work activities without **supervision**.

### ✨ 升级到 Band 7+ 的写法（使用替换词 + 更精确的搭配）

> Some argue that remote working can **enhance performance**, as employees avoid daily commuting and thus conserve valuable time and energy for professional tasks. On the other hand, critics contend that it may **undermine effectiveness**, since individuals are more likely to be **distracted by household responsibilities** and struggle to maintain focus. In my view, both perspectives are valid, yet in many occupations the absence of direct **supervision** tends to lead to reduced **output**, which explains why several multinational companies have recently required staff to return to the office.

### 🔍 对比解析

- **Productivity / efficiency → performance / effectiveness / output** ✅ 提升词汇 variety
- **workers → employees / staff / individuals** ✅ 避免重复
- **commute → daily commuting** ✅ 搭配更自然
- **truth in both views → both perspectives are valid** ✅ 更地道的表达
- **spend time on non-work activities → distracted by household responsibilities** ✅ 更准确

两段意思基本一样，但第二段因为词汇 variety + collocation 准确度高，整体感觉更自然、更“学术”，能拉到 Band 7+。
