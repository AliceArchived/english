---

literally

1. in a way that uses the ordinary and usual meaning of a word 按字面意义，字面上
   - He took her comments **literally**. 他按字面意思理解她的评论。
2. used to stress that a statement or description is true and accurate even though it may be surprising 确实，真的。强调尽管令人惊讶但确实真实。
   - He was quite **literally** jumping up and down in his rage. 他发怒的时候真的是上蹿下跳。
3. in a completely accurate way 确切地
   - The story he told was basically true, even if it wasn't **literally** true. 他讲的事虽然并不完全真实，但基本属实。
4. used in an exaggerated way to emphasize a statement or description that is not literally true or possible（用于夸张地强调）简直
   - She was **literally** beside herself with rage. 她简直是气得发疯。
